# The Answer — contact rule revised: physical contact is the only control channel

> **Canon revision only. No outcome work, no ranking, no coverage run, no implementation.**
> Supersedes the contact provisions of `_answer_visibility_contact_and_risk.md` §3.

---

# 1. The rule

> **Julian can influence his entrusted Answer only by maintaining surreptitious physical contact with it.
> He has no remote control channel.**

**If another person is physically holding the Answer and Julian is not in physical contact, its enchantment
obeys that holder's alignment.**

## 1a. A wish is not a second channel

**Julian may try to influence it with a wish.** A normal wish **cannot** overcome the Answer's far more
intricate enchantment, and **cannot redirect its response away from the person physically holding it.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **a wish aimed at the Answer is NOT an alternative interference path** | it does not become a way to control the manifestation |
| **and it does NOT make the manifestation non-probative** | an attempted wish is not doubt cast on the reading |

> **This narrows a rule I had written too widely.** The non-probative trigger is **the contact condition being
> unestablished** — **not** the mere possibility of interference, and **not** a wish having been aimed at the
> relic. Someone wishing at the Answer and failing leaves the reading exactly as sound as it was.

**Kept separate from the Veilweave.** That cloak's resistance to ordinary wishes is a different, still
**non-operative** record. The two are not the same mechanism and are not to be merged.

---

# 2. What follows

## 2a. The court case — the reading is of the holder

**In a court or hearing setting, where Julian cannot secretly maintain contact, the physical holder is the
alignment subject.**

This is the important consequence, and it points the other way from the caution I had been accumulating: **the
relic is dependable precisely where it matters most.** A formal proceeding is the setting least compatible
with surreptitious contact, so it is the setting where the manifestation most reliably reads the person
holding it.

> **Terminology note.** "Court/testimony setting" describes **the human proceeding.** The ban on calling the
> Answer's shape *testimony* stands — the relic never testifies; people testify, and a relic changes shape
> nearby.

## 2b. What it still cannot do — unchanged

**The manifestation reveals only present clarity, uncertainty, or evasiveness.** It does not reveal **factual
truth, guilt, historical causation, or responsibility for the twist**, and **no scene may convert present
alignment into a verdict about past facts.**

## 2c. Contact · accusation · finding — unchanged

**Actual concealed contact**, **an accusation of interference**, and **a finding** remain three distinct
things. **An accusation alone proves neither contact nor manipulation.**

---

# 3. The answer to the question that was left open

**"May any outcome rely on the Answer's manifestation at all?"** — **Yes, conditionally.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **an outcome may rely on the manifestation** | **only when the scene explicitly establishes the relevant physical-contact condition** |
| **it may then rely on** | **the holder's present alignment** |
| **it may never** | convert that alignment into a verdict about past facts |

**The declaration requirement stands, with its trigger corrected:** `answer.julian_active_contact` must be
established for the form to be relied on. **Undeclared is still not "absent."** What has changed is that
**establishing it is now a normal, reachable thing for a scene to do** — in a formal proceeding it is close to
the default — rather than a near-impossible precondition.

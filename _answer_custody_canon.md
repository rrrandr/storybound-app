# 1. Canon recorded (Roman, 2026-09-29)

## 1a. Entrustment

**The Answer is entrusted to the highest-ranking First Favored for ceremonial and judgement rites.** Office
holders who are also high-status warriors and generals may be entrusted with **one of the many Answers in
existence**.

**Julian holds the entrustment of this specific Answer by virtue of his office.** **A Dohkar presiding at a
First Sacrifice is too junior to hold one**, and **the presiding Dohkar has no custodial authority over the
relic** — not before this outcome, not after it.

> **This overturns the basis I wrote yesterday.** `_custody_bound_basis_trace.md` grounded Lirael's holding in
> `auth.dohkar_claims_rite_and_ruling`. **That was wrong** — it gave a junior office custody it cannot have,
> and it collapsed rite authority into relic authority, which is the exact collapse being corrected here.
> Superseded by §3.

## 1b. The four layers — kept separate, permanently

| layer | what it is | who |
|---|---|---|
| **instance** | this Answer is a **specific, stable relic instance** among many Answers | — |
| **title** | who owns it | the Institution *(`the_answer_institutional`)* |
| **entrustment / custody** | ceremonial charge of **this instance**, held **by virtue of office** — **not personal ownership** | **Julian** |
| **rite authority** | governs the **current rite and ruling** — and **does not thereby own, hold, or become entitled to the Answer** | the presiding Dohkar |
| **physical holding** | a **separately declared temporary act**, never inferred from any of the above | Lirael, for this questioning |

> **"Nobody owns it" is compatible with Julian having been entrusted with it.** Title and custody are
> different layers, and collapsing them is what produced yesterday's error.

**This also resolves an apparent tension in the source.** `231366` has *"a Dohkar or Chayr **holds** the
Answer"* during an AQ examination — **that is the physical-holding layer**, an act within a proceeding. It is
not entrustment, and it never implied a Dohkar could be the entrusted custodian.

## 1c. Veilweave — recorded, **non-operative here**

**The Veilweave befuddles physical attacks by projecting the wearer into multiple ethereal states of
superposition, which also makes the wearer very difficult to affect by ordinary wishes** — a wish directed at
them has trouble finding purchase, because their position in reality keeps changing. The same high
officials and warriors entrusted with an Answer are often entrusted with one of the legendary Veilweave
cloaks.

> **Recorded as canon; deliberately NOT operative for this outcome.** It is not cited, not relied on, and
> changes nothing below. **Flagged for later**, because "difficult to affect by ordinary wishes" is a
> potential limit on *any* outcome targeting a cloak-wearer — **and Julian is exactly the profile described.**
> That needs its own mechanism pass before it governs anything.

# 2. Re-authored — `unm.custody_severed` · ask-class `silence`

## 2a. What the grant reaches, chosen deliberately

With the layers separated, the `silence` ask must pick **one**. **Ending title alone is nearly inert** — the
operative relationship in this room is Julian's entrustment and Lirael's temporary holding, and neither
depends on who owns the thing. **The grant therefore reaches the entrustment.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **class** | `materialization_possession` (`191281`) |
| **`temporalOperation`** | **`current_state_transition`** — the entrustment **ends now.** Julian **was** entrusted, and that stands in history. |
| **`requiresActive[]`** | `answer.entrusted_to_julian` |
| **`ceases[]`** | **`answer.entrusted_to_julian`** — **alone** |
| **`establishes[]`** | **`answer.entrustment_vacant`** — *No First Favored holds ceremonial entrustment of this Answer. The charge is vacant, and this outcome confers it on no one.* |

## 2b. `unchanged[]` — and the non-collapse is the point

| atom | why it stays |
|---|---|
| **`answer.owned_by_institution`** | **title is a different layer.** Nobody's custody, and the Institution's ownership, are not the same question. |
| **`answer.instance_identity`** | it is still this Answer |
| **`answer.handed_to_lirael_for_questioning`** | **a completed act.** It happened; ending a custody does not un-hand it. |
| **`auth.dohkar_claims_rite_and_ruling`** | **and it confers nothing over the relic.** Stated explicitly so the collapse cannot recur: **the Dohkar does not acquire the Answer, may not claim it, and gains no say in its keeping.** |
| every other atom | — |

## 2c. Who may physically hold it during the present questioning

**Lirael — and on one explicit basis: `answer.handed_to_lirael_for_questioning`, the completed act by which
it came into her hands for this questioning.**

**Not** on the Dohkar's authority. **Not** on anyone's live custody, because there is none. **Her holding
rests on a historical act, not on a standing anyone currently holds** — which is precisely what makes it
temporary and precisely what makes its end a problem.

| may | hold it for the duration of **this questioning**, and use it as the questioning requires |
|---|---|
| **may not** | claim it · carry it away · use it outside this questioning. **It is still not hers** — and now it is not anyone's charge either. |

## 2d. What happens when the questioning ends

> **The act that put it in her hands is spent, and there is no entrusted holder to return it to.**

Julian's charge is vacant. The Institution holds title but **title is not custody** and this outcome confers
custody on no one. The Dohkar has authority over the rite and **explicitly none over the relic**.

**So at the end of the questioning a True Answer is in the hands of the woman whose conduct is being
questioned, and the order has no one standing to take it back.**

**This outcome does not resolve that**, and says so. **Nothing may infer a resolution from the silence** — not
a reversion to the Institution, not the Dohkar stepping in, not Julian resuming. A later scene must author it.

## 2e. Cost

**`custody_unclaimed`**, now meaning exactly: **no First Favored holds the ceremonial charge of this Answer.**
Not that the room is unsupervised — the rite proceeds under the Dohkar as before. **What is unattended is the
relic, and the person holding it is the accused.**

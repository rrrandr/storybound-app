# 3. Canon — what the Answer actually does

> **The Answer is not a lie detector.** It **may not** be used to establish **historical causation, factual
> guilt, or who caused the twist.**

**It responds to the present holder's inner alignment**, and it responds by **changing shape**:

| the holder | the shape |
|---|---|
| **clear belief / clear purpose** | a **straight, sharp blade** |
| **uncertainty** | a **scythe at one end and a mace at the other** |
| **evasiveness** | a **cat-o'-nine-tails**, or a whip |

**These are authored visual outcomes of current alignment — not a free-standing forensic capability.**

## 3a. What this settles, and what it opens

**It confirms the `unm.the_answer_speaks` block from explicit canon**, not merely from the prevents-clause at
`231513`. That outcome's grant was *"a reading that places the misalignment in Seren's own intent"* — **a claim
about historical causation, which the Answer may not make.** Blocked, now on the clearest possible ground.

> ### ⛔ The paragraph that stood here is WITHDRAWN — 2026-09-29
> It read that the relic *"is showing the whole assembly whether she is clear, uncertain, or evasive —
> continuously"*, and called it *"a mirror pointed at herself, and everyone can see it."* **"Showing the
> assembly", "continuously" and "everyone can see" all smuggle in a public verdict.**

**The correct statement:** while Lirael holds it, **the Answer visibly changes shape in her hands according to
her inner alignment.** People present **may see its physical form and draw their own conclusions.** It does
not address the room, declare anything, or bind anyone's judgement — and it **cannot** tell observers whether
she is factually right, guilty, lying about the past, or responsible for Seren's wish.

**Any social consequence is authored through what witnesses make of the visible shape**, never smuggled in as
an objective verdict from the relic. **The word "testimony" is not to be used of it.** See
`_answer_visibility_contact_and_risk.md`.

# 4. The `silence` ask, reopened

*"Don't let them ask about the Answer."* · *"It isn't mine, don't bring it up."*

**The custody reading is dissolved.** Nothing about ownership or charge is at stake — Julian is entrusted, she
is authorized, and the authorization expires on its own terms. **What the player is actually exposed to is
§3: the shape in her hands changes with her present alignment, and people may see it and draw conclusions.**
Not a verdict, and not testimony — **an exposure to other people's inference.**

## Three candidate directions, assessed — **none drafted**

| direction | class | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **stop the relic responding** | — | ⛔ **no carrier.** No class alters a relic's nature; this would be inventing a capability, which is the failure mode the mechanism-first rule exists to stop. |
| **make the room not see it** | `memory_perception` | ⚠ **this is the `unseen` ask**, already answered by `unm.the_turning_forgotten`. Folding `silence` into it would answer a different sentence — a T4 violation. |
| **the handoff never happened** | `history_rewrite` | ⚠ **supported, but it is not `silence`.** It ceases `answer.handed_to_lirael` — an `undo`-shaped grant, and a large one: if she never held it, the questioning itself was a different event. |

## Recommendation — **leave `silence` UNCOVERED**

**I have no supported, genuinely favorable effect for this ask that leaves Julian's entrustment intact.** The
one direction with a real carrier belongs to another ask-class, and the other two are an invented capability
and an answer to a different sentence.

> **The honest position is a third uncovered ask-class, not a manufactured outcome.** `unseen`, `escape` and
> now `silence` are uncovered — and the reason is the same each time: **the custody-shaped and
> forensic-shaped outcomes I drafted were leaning on capabilities this world does not have.** The lore did not
> narrow the design; it revealed that part of the design was never supported.

**Decision yours.** If `silence` should be covered, the ask itself may need re-reading — what a player means
by *"don't bring it up"* when the object in her hands visibly changes with her own clarity is a different
question from custody, and worth asking before anything is drafted.

> **And a further complication now stands between the ask and any answer:** per
> `_answer_visibility_contact_and_risk.md`, **the shape may not be relied on at all unless
> `answer.julian_active_contact` is declared.** An outcome built on what the form shows would have to declare
> it — which is one more reason not to draft one yet.

# 1. Correction — the shape is visible behavior, not a public verdict

**I overstated this yesterday**, in `_answer_lore_and_silence_reopened.md` §3a, and the wording is withdrawn:

> ~~*"the relic in her hands is showing the whole assembly whether she is clear, uncertain, or evasive —
> continuously, visibly… She is not holding evidence. She is holding a mirror pointed at herself, and
> everyone can see it."*~~

**"Showing the assembly", "continuously", and "a mirror everyone can see" all smuggle in a public verdict.**
The relic does not address the room, does not declare anything, and does not bind anyone's judgement.

## 1a. Terminology — two roles, never conflated

| term | meaning |
|---|---|
| **apparent physical holder** | the person **visibly holding** the Answer |
| **alignment subject** | the person whose **inner alignment the Answer is actually responding to** |

| declared contact | the relationship between them |
|---|---|
| `answer.julian_active_contact: absent` | **the apparent physical holder IS the alignment subject** |
| **active contact declared as materially affecting the manifestation** | **do NOT infer that the apparent holder is the alignment subject** |
| **the effect of contact is not authored** | **the form is non-probative for consequence selection** |

> **Trigger corrected 2026-09-29:** non-probative follows from **the contact condition being unestablished** —
> **not** from the possibility of interference, and **not** from a wish having been aimed at the relic. An
> attempted wish that fails leaves the reading exactly as sound as it was.

> **Why the split is required.** Saying *"the Answer responds to the current holder"* and then admitting
> Julian can influence it through concealed contact makes the base rule and its exception contradict each
> other. **Separating the roles keeps the normal handoff rule intact while making Julian's interference
> legible** — the exception narrows *who the subject is*, rather than breaking the rule about what the relic
> does.

## The correct boundary

| the Answer **may** | the Answer **cannot** |
|---|---|
| **visibly change shape** in the **apparent physical holder's** hands, according to the **alignment subject's** inner alignment | tell observers whether the alignment subject is **factually right** |
| — clear belief / clear purpose → a straight, sharp blade | tell them whether they are **guilty** |
| — uncertainty → a scythe at one end, a mace at the other | tell them whether they are **lying about the past** |
| — evasiveness → a cat-o'-nine-tails, or a whip | tell them who is **responsible for Seren's wish** |

**People present may see its physical form and draw their own conclusions.** That is all.

> **Any social consequence is authored through what witnesses make of the visible shape — never smuggled in as
> an objective verdict from the relic.** A scene in which the room reads a whip and concludes *she is lying*
> is authoring an inference by fallible people. A scene in which the whip *establishes* that she lied is
> using a forensic capability the Answer does not have.

**The word "testimony" is not to be used of the Answer's shape.**

# 3. New layer — apparent possession is not control

> ### ⚠ REVISED 2026-09-29 — see `_answer_contact_rule_revised.md`
> **Surreptitious physical contact is the ONLY control channel. Julian has no remote channel.** If another
> person is physically holding the Answer and he is not in contact, **its enchantment obeys that holder's
> alignment**. He may attempt a wish, but **a normal wish cannot overcome the Answer's far more intricate
> enchantment or redirect its response away from the physical holder** — and **a wish aimed at the relic is
> neither an interference path nor a reason to treat the manifestation as non-probative.**
>
> **Consequence:** the manifestation is probative **only where the scene establishes physical separation, or
> supervised handling that makes concealed contact impossible.** A formal proceeding is **not itself** a
> guarantee that Julian lacks contact — it is a **likely staging** for that condition, never a substitute for
> establishing it. The section below stands, read under this narrowing.

**Recorded as a limited Julian tactic, not a universal remote-control rule.**

> **Julian may maintain subtle physical contact with his entrusted Answer while another person appears to hold
> it, allowing him to keep control despite the apparent transfer of possession.**

## 3a. Three things, modelled separately

| layer | atom | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| **entrustment** | `answer.entrusted_to_julian` | his **office-bound charge** |
| **apparent physical possession** | `answer.apparent_possession` | who **visibly holds** it — presently Lirael |
| **active contact / control** | **`answer.julian_active_contact`** | whether Julian **is or is not** maintaining the contact needed to direct it |

**A handoff is not an automatic transfer of control.** Possession and control are now separate questions, and
neither answers the other.

## 3b. The declaration requirement

> **While Julian's active contact is present, the Answer's shape is NOT a clean readout of Lirael alone.**
> **Lirael is then the apparent physical holder, and who the alignment subject is may not be inferred.**

**Any scene or consequence relying on what the form reveals must declare `answer.julian_active_contact`:**

| value | meaning |
|---|---|
| `absent` | Julian is not maintaining contact |
| `visible` | he is, and it is apparent to those present |
| `concealed` | he is, and it is not |
| plus | **whether it is materially affecting the manifestation** |

**Undeclared is not "absent."** If the value is not established, **the shape may not be relied on** — by an
author, by a planner, or by a consequence. This is the no-inference-from-absence rule arriving in the one
place where it protects the player rather than the world.

## 3c. Deliberately narrow

**This establishes exactly one dangerous possibility: Julian can make an apparent handoff while retaining
leverage over his own relic.** It does **not** establish unlimited remote control, retroactive alteration, or
a general ability to falsify any reading. **Its mechanics are unauthored and it governs nothing until they
are.**

# 4. The risk boundary — why the tactic is exceptional

> **If Julian is caught maintaining contact to influence the Answer while someone else appears to hold it, it
> can cost him both his Chayr position and his entrustment of the Answer.**

**That is what makes it a high-stakes abuse of entrusted authority rather than a clever default move.** He
uses it only when the stakes justify risking the office and the relic.

## 4a. Three things kept distinct — permanently

| | |
|---|---|
| **actual concealed contact** | **a fact.** It must be **explicitly established**; it is never inferred from behaviour, from the shape, or from an allegation. |
| **an accusation of interference** | **a procedural/social claim.** It may be made **whether or not contact occurred.** |
| **a finding** | **a later determination** that may affect his office or his entrustment. |

## 4b. The rules that follow

1. **An accusation never proves the contact.**
2. **Neither an accusation nor a visible form of the Answer removes Julian from office or takes the relic from
   him.** Not automatically, not eventually, not by accumulation.
3. **Those consequences require their own authored procedural route** — a finding, reached by a declared
   process. **None exists yet**, and none may be improvised.
4. **`accusation.julian_interference` is a different fact from `accusation.family_accused`.** They are not
   interchangeable and neither implies the other.

# 5. What this creates, without inventing an outcome

**A live pressure with three moving parts, none of which is a Tempt grant:**

- **Lirael can be read through the Answer** — read, not proven; by people, not by the relic; **and only while she is the alignment subject, not merely the apparent physical holder.**
- **Julian can potentially manipulate the situation** — at a cost he would rarely pay.
- **An allegation against him is dangerous precisely because it may be true or false**, and nothing in the
  scene settles which.

**No outcome is added, changed, ranked, or covered.** The pressure is structural, and it is available to
ordinary authoring the moment the contact mechanics are written.

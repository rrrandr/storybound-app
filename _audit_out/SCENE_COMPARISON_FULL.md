## Writing improved — Scene 2 only

**Scene 2: yes.** It reads as continuous narration. "The Dohkar's voice cuts through the clearing
like a blade drawn too late" carries an image with a turn in it; "Julian has not moved, yet the air
bends toward him as if the clearing itself waits" holds two movements in one breath. The previous
version's "I turn. The grass parts under my feet." / "The assembly makes room. No one reaches for
me." is a list of captions by comparison.

**Scene 3: no.** It is longer per beat and still reads as disconnected statements. Three specific
faults, none of them about length:

1. **Three lines are recycled verbatim from its own Scene 2, and re-atomised.** Scene 2 had
   "Julian has not moved, yet the air bends toward him as if the clearing itself waits" as ONE
   compound sentence. Scene 3 reuses the same material split into three short beats:
   "Julian has not moved." / "The air bends toward him as if the clearing itself waits." /
   "His stillness pulls the assembly's gaze." The longer-sentence instruction did not stop the
   recycling, and the material arrives WORSE than where it came from.
2. **A prompt exemplar reached the reader verbatim.** The decision line was
   "Tell Devorah about the email, or wait until morning?" — a newsroom dilemma from an unrelated
   story shape, copied from the first item of an example list in the shared instructions. Fixed in
   this commit at all five sites.
3. **Writing-direction language in the protagonist's own thought:** "I make the wish small and in
   character". "In character" is a note to an author, not something a person thinks about herself.
   Also "The Dohkar's corruption spreads through the ritual's observation", which barely parses.

## What this does not establish

Late-story continuity, scenes beyond 3, or whether Scene 2's improvement holds across runs. One
sample, two scenes, one run. The three Scene 3 faults are craft and contamination problems that
the pacing instructions were never going to address.

## Five defects read in the text

1. **Scene 5 gives Julian the Dohkar's line AND role.** `JULIAN: "The rite is mine to preside. The
   memory is mine to answer for."` — "The rite is mine to preside" is the Dohkar's own line from
   Scene 2, and presiding is his office. Julian cannot preside the rite.
2. **Scene 10: "The clock advances."** Wrong register for a tribunal on spiralgrass — and the kind
   of contemporary-setting phrasing the cross-story cleanup was about.
3. **Scene 10 states its dilemma three times**: "I could speak the second handprint aloud. / Or I
   could let his denial stand." then "I must accuse him or let the denial stand." then "I must
   choose between breaking protocol for my ward or protecting my own standing."
4. **Dialogue punctuation inconsistent within the issue again**: Scene 2 is unquoted
   (`THE PRESIDING DOHKAR: Sacrificiant. Remain.`), Scenes 3–10 use quotes.
5. **The speaker label drifts**: `THE PRESIDING DOHKAR` (2, 6) / `PRESIDING DOHKAR` (4) /
   `DOHKAR` (8, 9) for the same character.

Minor: Scene 10 reports the Dohkar's non-action four times; "The clearing holds its breath" opens
both 5 and 7.

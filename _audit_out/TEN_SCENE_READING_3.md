The clearing has not moved since the rite ended. The spiralgrass still holds the warmth of every offering pressed into it.

Seren remains kneeling where the wish left her, composed, her whole face turned toward the Dohkar.

The presiding Dohkar lifts one hand without looking at me, the gesture of a man finishing a task he never wanted.

THE PRESIDING DOHKAR: She will answer for her conduct in preliminary review.

Two figures step from the assembly at his word. They do not hesitate.

Julian moves before they reach me. His hand finds the edge of The Answer again, anchoring it between us.

JULIAN: This matter concerns me as well.

The assembly shifts. No one speaks the accusation aloud, yet every eye measures the space between my hand and his.

THE PRESIDING DOHKAR: Bring her.

The witnesses reach for me. I feel the choice rise in my throat before the words can form.

I realize that old tarot deck is still in my hand. Reassuringly solid. Present. One of the heavy gold cards slips out — “Petition Fate” is inscribed in it.

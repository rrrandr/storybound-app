## But the modal is not reachable for the reader — a defect

The modal gains `.active` and computes `display: flex`, yet it renders **0×0 at opacity 0** and
cannot be clicked. The ancestor chain says why:

```
#intimacyExitModal    display=flex    vis=visible  opacity=0   box=0x0
#menuOverlay          display=none    vis=visible  opacity=1   box=0x0
.oas-active           display=block   vis=visible  opacity=1   box=1280x827
```

**`#intimacyExitModal` is parented inside `#menuOverlay`, which is `display: none`.** In
`public/index.html` the modal is at line 179 and `#menuOverlay` opens at line 100, with 12 opening
and 11 closing `<div>` between them — the overlay never closes, so the modal is its descendant.
A `position: fixed` element inside a non-rendered ancestor keeps its computed `display` but gets no
box, which is exactly what was measured.

**Consequence:** a reader who types `stop` sees nothing, cannot choose an exit, and the exchange
continues. So the route exists and fires, and still does not give the reader a usable exit — for a
different and more specific reason than the one I gave. This is the same bug class as the fate deck
dealt into a hidden mount.

**Not fixed.** Moving the modal out of `#menuOverlay` is a one-element relocation in shared markup,
and the unbalanced `<div>` around line 100-179 wants checking at the same time. That is a product
change for Roman to approve, not something to slip into this check.

The control now asserts the defect, so it fails loudly if the modal ever becomes visible and the
assertions need inverting.

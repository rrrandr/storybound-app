## 3 — SEREN'S WISH AND THE GOVERNING FAILURE

GOVERNING FAILURE [CANON, single and sufficient]: MISALIGNMENT OF MOTIVE. The wish
performs care while the desire held is self-display, and it is described too loosely

THE ONE CONCRETE IMPENDING CONSEQUENCE [PROPOSAL, filling the gap 3f1212c6 left]:
Fate honours the words and denies the intent. The words ask that the loss be FOUND;
the intent is that Seren be seen as kind. The nearest bargain Fate recognises
therefore serves the words in a way that serves the real desire — the loss is made

  WHAT BEGINS TO HAPPEN, by an ordinary route and with nothing manifesting: an elder
  guest present at the rite — one of the few who knows what became of her father —
  begins to say it aloud. This is exactly the canon mechanism: "someone present says

  starts to speak her father's name.
  WHY IT IS RUINOUS: it arrives in the same minute her supervision is being judged,

  WHAT JULIAN RECOGNIZES, from what he can actually perceive: he hears a misaligned

## 4 — JULIAN'S INTERVENTION

HIS OWN INDEPENDENT BARGAIN [PROPOSAL]. Not a subsidy, not a payment into Seren's
account, no contact with her wish at all. He makes his own wish, as himself, naming
his own sacrifice, against A SPECIFIC DANGER HE CAN SEE: that what he knows be said
here. Wording in the granter's careful register — that the account of her father
remain unsaid in this clearing today.
  WHAT HE WANTS IT TO ACCOMPLISH: silence in this place, today. Narrow on purpose.
  WHAT IT ACTUALLY CHANGES: the elder's voice does not arrive. The route Fate was
    taking to honour Seren's words closes, so the honouring finds no road it can
    complete — the wish stops short of whatever it was turning into, and nothing comes
    of it. THAT IS THE CORRECTED CANON OUTCOME, reached without touching Seren's
    bargain.
  CONCRETE EXTERNALIZED ACTION, and it is an ACTION, not a trait: he lowers his head
    so his hair curtains his face and TRACES the wish with two fingers on the inside
    of his own left forearm, across the old scar. Canon permits sign/write/TRACE for a
    wisher who cannot be heard. Timing: in the beat between the loose words landing
    and the elder's breath.
  WHY IT ESCAPES NOTICE: every eye is on Seren and Lirael; his stillness is habitual
    and reads as composure; the curtain of hair is his known tell and hides nothing
    anyone is looking for.
  HIS SACRIFICE [PROPOSAL]: the memory of the person who told him what became of her
    father. He keeps the FACT and loses the SOURCE. Proportionate: he is buying the
    unsayability of a truth, and he pays with his own access to its origin.
    Why it matters to him: it was told to him in confidence by someone he cannot now
    remember, so he loses a person as well as a provenance.
  WHAT REMAINS OF SEREN'S WISH: the misalignment, heard by everyone; the loose
    wording, already spoken and unrepeatable; her named memory. Whether her offering
    was taken for a bargain that never completed is left open here, as 3f1212c6 left
    the harm open — I will not invent a second mechanism to settle it.
  WHY FORBIDDEN: interfering in another's First Sacrifice voids the rite's purpose —
    the youth must reveal herself through her OWN bargain — and he is the Provast, the
    highest officer present, so his interference is the gravest available.
  WHY HE ACCEPTS: the alternative is the public loss of the thing he has protected,
    in the minute Lirael's path is being judged.
  WHAT REMAINS FOR THE REVEAL: he cannot prove how he knows what he knows, because he
    paid the source away. At the inquiry he must assert without provenance. Plus the
    traced forearm, his unexplained stillness in that beat, and the obscuring wishes

## 6 — THE REUSABLE PREPARATION REQUIREMENT (smallest change)

[PROPOSAL] One author-side, seed-level `causalFoundation` block, decided ONCE per
story and PERSISTED, holding exactly eight fields:
    established_truth · belief_and_desire · attempted_bargain · governing_failure
    concrete_consequence · intervention_and_price · observable_result · repercussions
RULES THAT MAKE IT USEFUL RATHER THAN ANOTHER QUOTA:
  · It is a PRECONDITION on planning, not a beat template. No counts, no floors, no
    mandatory twist: an ordinary successful wish fills governing_failure with "none"
    and concrete_consequence with what it simply grants. Nothing acquires a rescue.
  · PERSISTENCE: it is seed/plan state, so the missing person, the sacrifice and the
    intervention are decided once and never reinvented per scene.
  · THREE SEPARATE LAYERS per field: OBJECTIVE TRUTH · WHO KNOWS IT · WHAT THE READER
    MAY LEARN NOW. Existing sealed boundaries are untouched.
  · THE LEAK PROBLEM, solved by shape rather than by trust: the author receives ONLY
    observable_result and the perceptible part of concrete_consequence. It never
    receives intervention_and_price. So the author can stage "the elder draws breath
    and no words come, and no one can say why" WITHOUT being told that Julian traced a
    wish and paid a source away. The sealed fields stay author-side, exactly as
    sealedEvents already works.

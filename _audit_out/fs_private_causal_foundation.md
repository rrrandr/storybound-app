## 1.2 JULIAN'S INVOLVEMENT, AND WHY IT IS CULPABLE
[P] HE WAS AUGAR'S CHAYR, and present. The Chayr's office at a bargain is exactly this:
    HOLD THE LIMIT. Make the senior man bound his offer before he speaks it. That is
    what a second is for.
[P] HE DID NOT. Under time pressure, with the girl bleeding, he let an unbounded offer
    go — and, in the version of the memory he cannot stop rehearsing, he said HURRY.
    He will never be certain whether he said it. Nobody else can tell him, because the
    only other person there spent the capacity to remember it.
    This is the load-bearing fact of his character: Julian's catastrophe is not malice.
    It is a man who failed to bound a price once, under fear, and who does it AGAIN in
    Scene 1 with his own (1.4). The rhyme is the architecture. He knows precisely what
    an unbounded offering costs, because he watched one empty a man out, and in the beat
    after Seren's words land he leaves his own price open anyway.
[P] THERE IS NO OATH. Augar could not ask him for anything — he lost the capacity to ask
    in the same instant he lost everything else. Julian's silence was never a promise
    kept. It was his own, from the first hour, for himself.
[P] THE SECOND BARGAIN — THE CONCEALMENT WISH, its own account [C: 238180, accounts
    never merge]: within days, Julian wished that AUGAR'S SACRIFICE NEVER BECOME
    PUBLICLY KNOWN.
[P] HIS MOTIVE, which must survive an inquiry's reading and does not: any honest account
    of that hour says a Provast was destroyed by an unbounded offering, his second stood
    beside him and said nothing that would bound it, and the office was that second's
    inside the year. Julian has never been able to construct a telling of it that does
    not convict him, and he stopped trying. He also cannot prove he did not want the
    chair. Neither can anyone else. That unfalsifiability is the thing he actually fled.
[P] HIS PRICE FOR IT, named and bounded — he had time to think, and this is the one
    offering in the document he made carefully: THE BOND. [C: "bond" is a listed
    currency, 238181.] He gave up his attachment to Augar. Not the memory of it: he
    remembers every hour of twenty years of friendship in full detail and feels nothing
    when he does. That is how a man carries this for fifteen years without breaking.
    It is also why, in the clearing, his first instinct is not to protect Augar's
    daughter. There is nothing left in him that pulls that way.
    [P] POINTABLE LATER: he can describe Augar better than anyone alive and has never
    once said he misses him.

## 1.3 WHY THE MYSTERY SURVIVED FIFTEEN YEARS
This section exists because the directive is right that amnesia erases nothing public.
Augar's body, face, record, colleagues and history all still exist. The wish is narrow,
and naming its exact seam is what makes eventual exposure credible rather than granted.
[C] CIRCUMSTANCES, NOT MINDS (238179): Fate "does not install knowledge, skill, MEMORY,
    language or mastery — it arranges the improbable EVENTS by which you could earn or
    find them."
[C] FATE GOVERNS EVENTS, NEVER CONSENT (238180): the arrangeable list explicitly
    includes "a truth EXPOSED... a meeting... a diary that survives." Events only.
[P] SO THE CONCEALMENT WISH CANNOT: erase anyone's memory, install ignorance, make a
    person incurious, stop a question being asked, or hide Augar's body. It has no
    purchase on any mind at all. There is no "forgotten name makes you unfindable" rule
    in this story and I am not introducing one.
[P] WHAT IT ACTUALLY DOES: it arranges the ordinary events that keep the BARGAIN
    unrecorded. The inquiry closes for want of a complainant. The file is the incomplete
    copy. The one colleague who would have been on that road is reassigned the week
    before. The attestation is never countersigned, so it is never filed, so it is never
    read. Every link is individually unremarkable, and none of it is secrecy — it is a
    public record that never acquires the one page.
[P] THE SEAM, exactly: the wish protects THE SACRIFICE, not THE MAN, and it protects
    PUBLIC knowing, not private recognition. Anyone may find the man. What keeps not
    happening is anyone connecting a living stranger to a paid price. A daughter
    recognising her father's face is not a public knowing, and the wish cannot touch it.
    That is the exposure path, and it was built into Julian's own wording.
[P] WHERE AUGAR IS: not hidden. A nameless, unhurt First Favored was taken in, given a
    name, and has lived an ordinary documented life ever since, far from the
    Institution's traffic. People who knew Augar have been in the same province. The
    connection requires someone to expect a vanished Provast to be a living stranger,
    and that is the expectation the wish keeps arranging against.
[P] WHAT THE INSTITUTION TRIED: an inquiry into a vanished Provast. It established that
    he was gone and that no bargain was recorded against anyone. Unexplained, closed.
    Julian did not obstruct it. He did not have to.
[P] WHAT LIRAEL TRIED, and this is the part that hurts: she has wished. Repeatedly, for
    years, as a girl and as an apprentice. ORDER ③ REVELATION — "uncover what is already
    true... usually WELCOMES" — so Fate did not resist her. It answered the only way the
    law permits: "a knowledge wish is THE START OF AN ADVENTURE, NEVER A SHORTCUT." Each
    wish bought her a real opportunity — a road, a name, an archive, a witness. Each
    opportunity then failed to close, in an ordinary way: the witness died first, the
    file was the incomplete one, the man she was sent to had been reassigned. Her wishes
    and Julian's standing arrangement meet Fate's law separately and the outcomes fall
    out as luck and coincidence [C: NO VISIBLE WISH-DUELS, 238187]. Nobody is duelling.
    She has simply been unlucky in a way that is not luck.
[P] AND WHAT SHE PAID: memory, the decorous currency (1.0), over and over. She has been
    spending her memories of her father to look for her father. She can no longer
    clearly recall his face. The belief that he TOOK THE DECK (1.5) is partly a
    reconstruction that grew into the gap she paid to open.
    THIS IS THE EXEMPLAR for 1.8: the shortcut was available, lawful and welcomed, and
    its fulfilment arrives through consequences that defeat its own purpose.
[P] WHICH IS ALSO WHY SHE WANTS AN ANSWER AND NOT A PERSON [C: aboutToHappen (4) — "she
    mistook what you actually wanted (an answer) for what she asked Fate to give you (a
    person)"]. She has spent too much of him to still be reaching for him.

## 1.5 THE DECK
[C] Her GREAT-grandmother's tarot deck, not her grandmother's. A true relic,
    recognisable on sight to any First Favored, in the company of The Answer and
    Veilweave.
[P] WHERE IT IS: in an overlooked fold of the family's stored ceremonial sash, where
    AUGAR put it. He had it with him that day. In the minutes between the wish and the
    emptying he folded it away, the way a man puts something safe without thinking.
[P] WHY HE HAD IT OUT: nobody knows, and nobody ever will, because the only mind that
    knew was spent. This is the shape of the whole book in one object — a question Fate's
    own collection made permanently unanswerable.
[P] HER BELIEF, sincerely mistaken and preserved: she believes her father took the deck
    when he went. He did take it. He did not take it AWAY. The family's shame is grief
    over a lost relic, not guilt about blaming him; she hid nothing and let nobody be
    blamed.
[P] NO CONJURING, NO TRANSPORT, NO HANDOVER, NO CHILDHOOD OFFICIATING. The deck has been
    in that sash for fifteen years. Fate's entire contribution is THE DISCOVERY.
[P] THE GARMENT, reconciled with First Favored clothing canon [C: the gossamer tunic is
    the SOLE GARMENT; the officiating Sacrificiant adds "ONE ornamented ceremonial
    SASH"]: it is the FAMILY's sash, stored in a chest since Augar's day. Today is the
    first time Lirael wears it, because today is the first time she officiates. Nobody
    has worn it since he folded something into it.
[P] NO TELEGRAPHING: the sash is established as weight, and as the thing she tightens
    for composure, never as a container. The fold goes unmentioned until she feels it.

[P] THAT PREDICTION IS WRONG, in magnitude and in target — 1.4 threatens nobody. He is

[P] HIS WISH, his own account: that the loss she finds be ANOTHER one — something lost
    ENOUGH TO TURN HER FROM HIM.
[P] "ENOUGH" IS HIS OWN BENDING WORD. A trinket is also "another loss"; what makes the

    catastrophe. A witness could later point at "enough".
    NO PRICING LAW IS INVOLVED. The size of the answer follows from the WORDS, not from

## 1.8 THE GENERALISED PLANNING REQUIREMENT  (applies to every future magical mystery)
Before outlining a magical crisis, establish WHY THE CHARACTERS CANNOT SIMPLY WISH IT
SOLVED — from the situation, never from a blanket prohibition. The legitimate sources:
  COST ............ what it would take from this particular person, now.
  CULTURAL PRACTICE what this people considers decorous to offer (1.0). Lawful and
                    indecent is a real constraint on a person with standing.
  TRAINING ........ who knows how to bound an offer, and who is too frightened to.
  RISK ............ ⑥⑦⑧ resist, and a resisted Order warps rather than refuses.
  MISALIGNMENT .... the wish answers the words, and the words are rarely the desire.
  CONFLICTING WISHES separate accounts, separate adjudications, outcomes falling out as
                    coincidence [C: NO VISIBLE WISH-DUELS].
  INCOMPLETE KNOWLEDGE you cannot phrase what you do not know you need.
ROUTINE WISHES REMAIN EVERYDAY: keeping tea hot, a fair wind, a mended cup [C: ② Fate
WELCOMES]. This world is not one where magic is withheld.
COMPLEX WISHES ARE POSSIBLE AND DANGEROUS: granted, and fulfilled through consequences
that defeat the shortcut's purpose. RECONCILED WITH THE KNOWLEDGE-WISH RULE [C: 238179]:
a revelation wish is welcomed and buys an ADVENTURE, not an answer — and 1.3 is the
worked exemplar, where the price of asking was the thing she was asking about.
NEVER ACCEPTABLE as an explanation: "wishing cannot do that" where no law says so.

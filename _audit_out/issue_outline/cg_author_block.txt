  VEILWOOD GEOGRAPHY (canon, and this scene is in the Veilwood — HARD):
    · Nothing in the Veilwood is built. There are no doors, doorways, doorframes, gates, arches, porches, vestibules or halls anywhere in it.
    · A Veilwood ceremony clearing is OPEN GROUND inside a ring of living trunks, open to the sky. An opening in that ring is a GAP, or a PASSAGE BETWEEN TREES — a space people walk through, trunks to either side, nothing overhead but canopy and sky. It is not a doorway and nothing hangs across it.
    · Veilwood tree HOMES have entrances covered by thick hanging canopies, which are drawn aside to pass. Those canopies belong to the homes. A ceremony clearing has none: it is not enclosed and has nothing to draw aside.
    · A clearing's own white weeping-willow veil-canopy hangs OVERHEAD from the mated-pair trees. That is the trees' foliage. It is not an entrance covering and it covers no opening.
    · To stop someone leaving a clearing you use its real geography: step into the gap between the trunks, stand in the seam, put a hand on a trunk, let the assembly close between them and the passage. Nothing is shut, barred, filled or leaned in.
    This rules out FIGURATIVE architecture in a Veilwood scene as well: no door closing on anyone, no doorway she fills, no room with one exit. There is no room. (A professional or a decision "threshold", meaning a milestone or a choice, is not architecture and is fine.)

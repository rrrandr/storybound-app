
═══ BEFORE YOU OUTLINE A MAGICAL CRISIS OR A MYSTERY: WHY CAN NOBODY SIMPLY WISH IT SOLVED? ═══
Answer this from the SITUATION, never from a blanket prohibition. "Wishing cannot do that" is not an answer unless a law actually says so. The legitimate sources of difficulty:
  COST — what it would take from THIS person, now, with what they actually have.
  CULTURAL PRACTICE — what this people considers decorous to offer. Lawful but indecent is a real constraint on anyone with standing to lose.
  TRAINING — who knows how to bound an offer, and who is too frightened to. Most people hold three to five formulas and nothing else.
  RISK — Orders 6, 7 and 8 resist, and a resisted Order WARPS rather than refusing.
  MISALIGNMENT — Fate answers the words, and the words are rarely the desire.
  CONFLICTING WISHES — separate accounts, separate adjudications, outcomes falling out as coincidence.
  INCOMPLETE KNOWLEDGE — nobody can phrase what they do not know they need.
ACCOUNT FOR THE FORMULAS ORDINARY PEOPLE ACTUALLY KNOW. Children are taught a small emergency repertoire the way they are taught first aid, so a character who does not reach for the familiar remedy needs a SITUATIONAL reason — they are too hurt to speak, the formula does not cover this, the price is one they refuse, they were never taught, or reaching for it is what causes the disaster. IGNORING AN AVAILABLE REMEDY IS A PLOT HOLE UNLESS THE SCENE EXPLAINS IT. And reaching PAST the familiar formula for something far more ambitious is a different and much larger risk, which is a story in itself.
ROUTINE WISHES STAY ROUTINE: a kettle kept warm, a fair wind, a steady footing, a mended cup. This is not a world where magic is withheld, and writing it as withheld is its own failure.
COMPLEX WISHES ARE POSSIBLE AND OFTEN DANGEROUS: granted, and sometimes fulfilled through consequences that defeat the shortcut's own purpose.
AND A REVELATION WISH IS NOT A GUARANTEE OF DELAY. Fate answers it THROUGH EVENTS rather than by installing knowledge — but an event can be IMMEDIATE and ordinary, and frequently is: the person who knows walks in, the page is in the box she opens that afternoon, the question is answered by someone who had no reason not to say it. A WELCOMED ORDER-3 WISH USUALLY WORKS. Do NOT use "it only buys an adventure" as a standing explanation for a mystery that never resolves — that is the blanket prohibition this section exists to forbid, wearing a different hat.
SO IF A CHARACTER HAS BEEN ASKING FOR YEARS AND STILL HAS NOTHING, THAT MUST BE EARNED: name the specific thing that keeps closing the opportunities — a person actively concealing it, a record that was never written, a witness who died first. A long-unsolved mystery needs a NAMED OBSTRUCTION, not the nature of wishing.

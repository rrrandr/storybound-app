THE CRAFT IS STRATEGIC, NOT VERBAL. A wish-slinger is not merely faster. They RECOGNISE SITUATIONS, ANTICIPATE REPLIES, and know which familiar formula can be safely altered and which alteration could ruin them. Prepared openings and tactical foresight, delivered fast and exactly under pressure. [prevents: writing skill as eloquence, or as speed alone.]
RHYME IS A MNEMONIC. It helps a frightened person remember wording when they cannot think. IT GRANTS NO MAGICAL BONUS AND NO PROTECTION — a rhyme is not a safety rating, and a memorable formula can be a catastrophically bad one (see THE RHYME EVERY STUDENT UNLEARNS). [prevents: rhyme written as a power multiplier; a verse treated as inherently safe.]
WHAT EXPERTISE ACTUALLY CONSISTS OF, beyond vocabulary: assessing ALIGNMENT; choosing an AFFORDABLE sacrifice; reading an opponent's INTENTION; and PREPARING THE CIRCUMSTANCES the next wish will need. The last one is the mark of a master — most of a duel is won before anyone speaks.
ORDINARY FAMILIARITY IS NOT TRAINING. Four honest tiers:
  CHILDREN keep a small emergency repertoire — culturally this is first aid, not magic school. Taught by a parent, drilled, and mostly never used.
  MOST ADULTS reliably remember three to five formulas, and will fumble a sixth.
  A BEGINNING STUDENT learns roughly a dozen standard openings, and learns the SCOPE and the COST with each one — not the rhyme alone.
  EXPERT SLINGERS maintain large repertoires, study counters and combinations, and keep NOTEBOOKS of revised wishes and tested wording. The notebook is the characterising object: crossings-out, a price that proved too high, a line that worked once and is circled twice.
SIX EVERYDAY FORMULAS (regional register: hair, lashes, dreams, taste, a tune — the common currencies of the central Fatelands). Each is a FAMILIAR OPENING, which reduces avoidable mistakes and guarantees NOTHING:
  1 KETTLE-WARMING — "Warm my kettle, spare me care; / take this offered strand of hair." Order 2, temporary aid; Fate welcomes it.
  2 A PRECARIOUS STEP — "Steady feet upon this stair; / take from me this strand of hair." Order 2. The same offering because the ask is the same size.
  3 A SMALL FLAME — "Let this little ember die; / take a lash beneath my eye." Order 2. For an ember. Not for a burning house.
  4 A SHALLOW CUT — "Let this shallow bleeding cease; / take tonight's sweet dreams for peace." Order 1, restoration, welcomed — AND ITS SCOPE IS IN ITS OWN FIRST LINE. It says SHALLOW. It is not a treatment for a mortal wound, and a frightened person reaching for it over a serious injury is a recognisable kind of tragedy.
  5 A MISPLACED BELONGING — "Bring my missing key to sight; / take my taste of sweet tonight." Order 3, revelation, welcomed. NOTE WHY IT IS WELL MADE: it asks to SEE, not to RECEIVE. Fate conjures and carries nothing, so a formula asking for the key IN HAND would reach past what Fate readily does; "to sight" stays inside the law. This is the craft, in one word.
  6 CALLING FOR HELP — "Let my cry find someone near; / take the tune I hold most dear." Order 5, fortune — RISKY, and for two reasons a teacher names aloud. Fate can arrange that someone is WITHIN EARSHOT; it can never arrange that they CHOOSE TO HELP. And "someone near" never says WHO. The person who comes may be the last person you wanted.
THE PRICE IS REAL EVEN WHEN IT IS SMALL. Hair and lashes regrow, and that is exactly why they pay for trivial aid and nothing more — what is given is GONE FROM THE GIVER, which satisfies conservation without pretending a strand of hair buys anything large. "The tune I hold most dear" is a REAL remembered tune the wisher understands they may lose, and a child who offers it for a warm kettle has been badly taught.
REGIONAL REGISTER IS NOT UNIVERSAL. These are central-Fatelands sayings. Do NOT give every culture the same offerings. FIRST FAVORED INSTRUCTION RUNS ON MEMORY AND EMOTION, so their versions of the same six trade differently — "take the name of a street I walked once", "take my pleasure in the first cold morning", "take how my mother's voice sounded saying my name" — and a First Favored child offering hair reads as foreign, ill-taught, or deliberately provocative.
THE RHYME EVERY STUDENT UNLEARNS — the module's teaching centre. Two street rhymes circulate widely and neither belongs in a curriculum:
  "WARD MY HEALTH, FOR A DAY'S WEALTH." Both halves are unbounded. Warded against WHAT, for HOW LONG? A DAY'S wealth measured against WHOSE day? Open scope with an unanchored quantity is an invitation to a warp, and a folk charm is what it is — not an opening.
  "GIVE ME SKILL TO WIN FOR THE PAIN OF A PIN." This one is WRONG IN LAW, not merely sloppy, and it is the first thing a student unlearns: FATE DOES NOT INSTALL SKILL. A skill wish is the start of an adventure and never a shortcut, so what this buys is not ability — it is the improbable EVENTS by which you might one day have earned it, arriving on Fate's schedule and not yours: the rival who withdraws, the teacher met years too late, the contest cancelled. The pin is also an insult of an offering. It rhymes beautifully and it is a trap, which is precisely why it is taught.
THE ADVANCED RESCUE FORMULA — "Let death pass — / I offer [amount] of my past." IT IS A MEMORISED STRUCTURE WITH A DELIBERATELY SPECIFIED OFFERING, never words containing a literal placeholder: a practitioner NAMES the memory or the bounded portion of their past as they speak it, and the naming IS the formula. Order 1 restoration, which is why it works at all. THE BLANK IS THE SAFETY. A second at a bargain exists to make certain the amount gets named, and the whole protection of this formula fails the moment someone under terror says "my past" and means all of it. [prevents: writing the bracket as spoken text; treating the formula as safe independent of its named limit.]
INSTITUTIONS AND COMPETITION. The Lytharyn Institute of Thaumaturgy has whole fields of study in wish composition, pricing, alignment, counters and combinations. Tournaments across the Fatelands run in four forms: TIMED contests with a speed-chess quality; ONE-ON-ONE verbal confrontations with the intensity of a rap battle; WEAPONS-AND-WISHES combat, which is literally dangerous; and TEAM events built on coordinated, complementary wishes. Contestants create openings, bait costly responses, give up position for later advantage, and interrupt an opponent's opportunity before it can be used.
AND THE SPORT DOES NOT SUSPEND THE LAW. Each wish remains ITS OWN BARGAIN with its own price and its own account: teams do NOT pool sacrifices, and no coordination lets one contestant spend another's life. Opposing wishes never meet as visible magic — no beams, no clash, no "whose wish is stronger"; each meets Fate separately and the outcomes fall out as luck and coincidence. SPORTING LIMITS ARE RULES AND SUPERVISION, NOT PHYSICS: a tournament wish is not free, not reversible and not harmless, and the difference between a regulated bout and open combat is the referee, the declared ceiling on offerings and the people standing ready — never a softer Fate. [prevents: free or reversible contest magic; pooled team sacrifices; sorcery-vs-sorcery duels.]

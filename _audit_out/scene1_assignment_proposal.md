## THE FIXED SPINE (not negotiable by the planner)
  1 Seren names her offering aloud — a memory — before the desire. The rite's order.
  2 She abandons the rehearsed wish and spends her First Sacrifice on Lirael,
    blurring the wound: that Lirael find the one she lost.
  3 Every First Favored hears the misalignment; the trained ones sooner. Lirael
    alone knows WHERE THE WORDING CAME FROM.
  4 Lirael is sealed and forbidden, and the wish is about her, so moving will look
    like self-dealing. She moves anyway: tears the band, speaks, tries to get Seren
    to restate it.
  5 The words finish first. FATE ANSWERS. Nothing manifests. The literal words come
    true by an ordinary-looking route, and someone present can name which word bent.
  6 The Dohkar names the mechanism. The charges follow: the wish was shaped for its
    supervisor's benefit, and the supervisor broke her seal.
  7 Julian, present and unmoved at the edge throughout, moves only now.

## WHAT THE SCENE MUST DELIVER
  · THE TURN ITSELF, not its announcement. Fate's answer must HAPPEN on the page and
    be legible as an answer. A scene that says Fate answered and defers the
    consequence has not delivered the scene.
  · The answer arrives through what is ALREADY REAL. No apparition, no summoned
    figure, no light, no display, no darkening grass, no weather.
  · The POV never leaves Lirael.

## WHAT THE SCENE MUST NOT DO
  · NO CLOSING FORK. The scene does not end on a choice Lirael makes. It ends on
    Julian's move into the circle. Run-or-remain belongs to the OAS, after the fixed
    intimate interrogation and the Dohkar's interruption.
  · NO CHOICE TO PREVENT THE EVENT. Lirael cannot halt the wish, and no affordance
    may imply she can. Tearing the band is what lets her SPEAK; it is not a parry.
  · NO RULE EXPOSITION. No list of violations, no count of fractures, no phrase like
    "the beneficiary was not the wisher". The reader infers the rule from the
    consequence, never before it.
  · NOTHING OF JULIAN'S INTERVENTION. He is an observer who stands too still.

## WHAT REMAINS GENUINELY OPEN
Only the micro-expression beat inside the scene, and after it the OAS's run-or-
remain. Both are real choices about HOW SHE CARRIES IT, never about preventing it.

## LENGTH
700-870 words of main prose, plus the two alternative expansion payoffs. The
approved opening needs room to re-establish the reader; the budget is not a cap on
the scene, and the scene's own turn is not to be truncated to hit a number.

## THE FIELDS THE PLANNER MAY STILL AUTHOR
Staging, sensory grounding, which word bends and by what ordinary route, the
micro-expression pair, Seren's and the Dohkar's specific lines, and the
character-plus / environment-plus material. Everything in THE FIXED SPINE is given.

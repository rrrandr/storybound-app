## PART 2 — THE DESIGN: duels out of established law

Almost nothing new is needed, because four established rules already produce a
quickdraw art.

  ESTABLISHED  EXTERNALIZATION — a wish must be GIVEN FORM (spoken, signed, written
    or enacted); "Fate does not read the mind."
      => A wish takes TIME TO UTTER. That time is the duel.
  ESTABLISHED  Fate honours the words AS SPOKEN and denies the intent AS MEANT;
    imprecision is punished "the instant the words finish."
      => PRECISION IS THE WEAPON, and the clock is your enemy. Hence composed
         verses, memorized verbatim, and notebooks to draft them in. RHYME IS A
         MNEMONIC for exact retention under pressure and an emerging art — it buys
         no power. A sloppy rhyme is worse than careful prose, because Fate will
         honour the rhyme you actually said.
  ESTABLISHED  Fate MANIFESTS NOTHING; it rearranges what is ALREADY REAL by an
    ordinary-looking route.
      => NO TRIVIAL INSTANT WIN. A wish cannot conjure a result; it must find a
         path through what is already in the room. The shorter and vaguer the
         one-line kill, the more exposed it is to being honoured literally against
         its speaker.
  ESTABLISHED  The wisher only OFFERS; FATE chooses the sacrifice within the band.
      => You cannot know your own price in advance, so you cannot safely spend
         wishes at speed. SACRIFICE, NOT SPEED, is the limiting resource.

### THE THREE SMALLEST NEW RULES

  N1  THE BINDING WINDOW. A wish is affectable only WHILE IT IS BEING GIVEN FORM.
      It binds when the words finish. (Barely new — aboutToHappen already says Fate
      answers the instant the words finish; this only names the interval before it.)
  N2  HALT AND RE-ALIGN is the licensed defensive technique, and it is a REAL
      SKILL, not an office: naming the bending word and supplying a tighter one
      before the sentence closes. It does not cancel a wish; it CHANGES WHAT THE
      WISH SAYS while it is still being said. This is the duellist's parry and the
      teacher's safety net, and it is already Lirael's assigned task. A parry is
      itself an utterance, so it costs time and can be beaten by a shorter line.
  N3  RE-AIMING IN FLIGHT. A wish may be spent to re-aim an answer Fate is already
      giving — not to undo it. Vanishingly rare, enormously priced, and the only
      new rule that is genuinely new. It exists because Julian's sealed truth
      requires it and nothing else in canon supplies it.

### WHY RESOLUTION TERMINATES
There is no stack, because there is no open wish to bid over. The window is the
length of a sentence; when it closes, Fate has answered. Reversing a landed result
is Order 8, HISTORY, "nearly impossible" — already closed by canon. So an exchange
is a RACE AGAINST THE END OF A SENTENCE, not a tower of cancellations. A duel ends
when someone's wording lands, or when a duellist has nothing left Fate will take.

### WHAT COMBAT THEREFORE REWARDS
Pre-composition, brevity with precision, reading your opponent's grammar early
enough to parry the right word, and a sober sense of what you can still afford to
lose. Speed matters, but a fast imprecise line is how duellists die — by their own
wish, honoured exactly.

### OPTIONAL, PROPOSED, WITH LIMITS — prepared practice grounds
A rite or ground whose protection was PAID FOR IN ADVANCE, binding only the SCOPE
of what a wish uttered inside it may rearrange. LIMITS, required if adopted: it is
a stored, EXHAUSTIBLE quantity, consumed per use and renewed by further sacrifice;
it bounds scope only and grants no immunity to anyone; it cannot protect its own
holder from a wish they themselves utter; and nothing prevents a wish from being
made outside it. Compatible with duels. Not needed by Part 3.

## PART 3 — SEREN'S RITE UNDER THIS DESIGN

WITHOUT JULIAN (proposal, filling 1.4's gap). "The one she lost" has several true
readings, and imprecision bends toward the heaviest one available in the room. The
heaviest is Lirael's dead. The hinge would have bent onto a reading whose honouring
demanded something irreversible of the living — the kind of answer an assembly
cannot undo and a Dohkar order cannot forgive.

WHAT JULIAN'S BARGAIN CHANGES. Not whether the wish twisted — it did, immediately,
in the room, exactly as canon requires. He re-aimed the hinge (N3) onto the lightest
true reading: the loss found as the assembly's own attention, or a man found who is
immediately gone. The twist stays pointable-at; someone can still say which word
bent. What he bought is the DIFFERENCE BETWEEN A DISASTER AND A HUMILIATION.
To a trained eye that is the tell: imprecision does not bend gently.

WHY FORBIDDEN AND COSTLY. Interfering in another's First Sacrifice is a crime
against the rite — the scene's own charges show how gravely it is read. He had no
standing to halt and re-align (N2 is the officiant's licensed act, and he was an
observer), so he had only N3, the rarest and dearest bargain there is. And it must
be concealed, which is itself a further debt — hence julian_obscuring_wishes.
It cannot solve later crises: he must be present, sense early, be unsealed, still
have something enormous to give, AND pay again to hide it.

WHY LIRAEL FAILS, all three reasons already canon, none of them inability:
  · SHE IS SEALED. The band stops her from wishing; to halt and re-align she must
    first tear it. Her own safety technique requires breaking her own seal —
    which is beat 6, and which is what makes her look guilty.
  · THE WISH IS ABOUT HER. aboutToHappen (5): intervening "will look like
    manipulating your own ward's rite for your own benefit." The conflict of
    interest costs her the moment she needed.
  · THE WINDOW IS ONE SENTENCE LONG, and Seren is already rushing the rest of it.
    She tore the band and spoke, and the words finished first.

WHAT WITNESSES PERCEIVE. Loose wording. The misalignment — every First Favored
senses it; the trained ones sooner. Lirael tearing her seal and moving. The twist
landing through an ordinary-looking route they can name. Fate manifesting nothing.
They do NOT perceive Julian's wish. They may notice only that he stood too still —
his canon tell, and a detail that re-reads at scene 16.

THE SECRET STAYS OUT OF PROSE. Nothing above reaches the author, the planner, the
reader, affordances or ledgers. julian_counter_wish and julian_obscuring_wishes stay
sealed to scene 16 with leak control at S19. The author needs only: the wish twists
immediately, Fate manifests nothing, the assembly misattributes, and the PC is
innocent.

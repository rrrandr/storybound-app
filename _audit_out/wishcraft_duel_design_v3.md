## PART 1 — THE DESIGN (smallest genuinely new content)

### What a duel is
Successive bargains. Each is externalised (spoken, signed, written, traced), each
names its own price out of the wisher's own life, each is answered separately by
Fate, and each arrives as luck or coincidence. No clash, no stack, no cancellation.

### What a landed wish does
NOT necessarily end the exchange. A landed wish produces a CONDITION — down,
winded, disarmed, exposed, blinded by grit — and conditions are answerable. So
duels are combinations: each move changes what is true, and the next move is priced
and worded against the new truth.

### The one distinction that keeps this bounded
  COUNTERACTING A PRESENT CONDITION is ordinary and cheap: Restoration (welcomes),
    Temporary-Aid (welcomes). "Let my breath come back" is a normal bargain.
  ERASING THE EVENT is Order 8, HISTORY, "nearly impossible". "Let me not have
    fallen" is not a move anyone makes twice.
  Already canon, both of them. Nothing new. This is what lets a duellist recover
  without anyone rewriting history, and it is why duels do not become undo-wars.

### Why it terminates
Not by a stack rule. By THE PRICE. Every move names a specific sacrifice out of a
finite life, and a miserly price buys a crooked result that can land on its own
speaker. A duel ends when someone is out of things they are willing to name, when
a crooked result turns on them, or when the conditions have accumulated past
recovery. Attrition of self, not depletion of mana.

### Why no trivial instant win
Three live rules, no new ones. Fate MANIFESTS NOTHING and only rearranges what is
already real, so a decisive wish needs an ordinary route present in the scene.
A lethal outcome demands a proportionate price, and underpaying buys crooked.
And phrasing craft exists because a badly worded decisive wish is the one most
likely to be honoured against the speaker.

### NOTEBOOKS AND RHYME — craft, no bonus
Fate honours words exactly, so precision is the weapon and pressure is its enemy.
Verse is retained verbatim; verbatim is what Fate requires. A notebook holds drafted
LINES and their INTENDED OFFERINGS, worked over at leisure — the same artisan skill
the live pricing rule already calls billable. Rhyme multiplies nothing: a sloppy
rhyme is worse than careful prose, because Fate honours the rhyme you actually said.
A slinger's edge is prepared precision and correct pricing, not speed alone.

### HALT AND RE-ALIGN, corrected (no new power)
The officiant's duty, app.js:134621. Since consent is mechanical, it CANNOT be
editing another's bargain. It is: stop the rite with voice and authority, and COACH
THE WISHER TO RESTATE THEIR OWN WISH — the youth re-words it herself, sincerely
adopting the corrected outcome, and names her own price. No consent question, no
payer question, no hijack. A supervisor coaches; they do not speak into the bargain.

### ONE GENUINELY NEW RULE, named as new
PAYING ANOTHER'S SHORTFALL. A third party may make their OWN wish, as themselves,
naming their own proportionate sacrifice, with another person as beneficiary, so
that the bargain Fate answers nearest is no longer the crooked one. It does not
cancel, edit, or reverse the first wish. It is the only new rule here, and it is
required because julian_counter_wish exists and nothing else in canon supplies it.
It is extremely dear precisely because the shortfall is measured against someone
else's underpriced wish, which the payer does not get to choose.

## PART 2 — THREE SUCCESSIVE MOVES

Two slingers on a wet road. Nothing manifests; everything looks like bad luck.

MOVE 1 — attacker, from the notebook, precise and cheaply priced.
  "I offer the name of my first horse: let his footing find the rain's own work."
  Fate's answer, by ordinary route: the camber, the wet, a slick of trodden grass.
  He goes down hard and is winded. CONDITION: down, breath gone, a beat behind.
  The exchange does NOT end.

MOVE 2 — defender, recovery. He does not try to un-fall; that is History, and he
  knows better than to ask.
  "I offer the taste of apples: let my breath come back to me now."
  RESTORATION / TEMPORARY-AID — welcomed, cheap, fast, and honestly priced. He is
  up. The fall still happened: it cost him tempo and a sacrifice he will not have
  again. He has answered a CONDITION, not edited the past.

MOVE 3 — attacker, combination, priced against what the fall created.
  The fall knocked the defender's notebook open into the wet. A bigger ask, so a
  bigger named price.
  "I offer my sister's lullaby: let the wind read what he has written."
  The pages blur. His rehearsed lines are gone, and with them his prepared
  precision. He can still wish, extempore, under pressure, into a law that punishes
  imprecision instantly. The duel is now decided by craft and nerve rather than by
  any wish being "stronger" — and it ends when one of them has nothing left he is
  willing to name.

Each move met Fate's law separately. No move cancelled another. Every outcome was
luck and coincidence.

## PART 3 — SEREN'S RITE UNDER THESE RULES

### WHAT GOES WRONG, concretely — and it is the PRICE, not only the wording
Seren named her offering FIRST, as the rite's fixed order requires: a memory, priced
for the REHEARSED self-directed wish she had worked on for months. Then she
substituted a different and far larger wish — FIND A PERSON — without changing the
price. By the live rule, that price is now MISERLY, and a miserly price buys A
LESSER OR CROOKED RESULT.
So there are TWO defects, and only one is the one everybody notices:
  the WORDING is loose ("the one you lost" has several true readings);
  the PRICE is short for the wish she actually made.

### WITHOUT JULIAN — one concrete proposed event
Fate answers the nearest bargain it recognises, and completes a short-paid one from
the nearest available source: the wisher's own life. Seren is a child with little to
give. The crooked honouring takes HER as the completion — she is found as the one
who is lost. By an entirely ordinary route and with nothing manifesting: she does
not rise from her knees. No apparition, no light, no summoned figure; the assembly
watches a girl fail to stand up at her own coming-of-age, and the words are honoured
exactly — Lirael's loss is found, because it has just been made.
That is the disaster. It is swift, lethal, and visibly magic-free.

### WHAT JULIAN'S BARGAIN CHANGES
He pays the shortfall (Part 1's one new rule). As himself, he makes his own wish,
names a proportionate sacrifice out of his own life, and takes Seren as beneficiary
— the exact granter/beneficiary structure of app.js:238336, where the granter is
"the one permanently diminished". The bargain Fate then answers nearest is no longer
crooked, so the honouring runs STRAIGHT instead: "find the one she lost" lands as the
assembly's own attention turning to Lirael — the loss made undeniable rather than
made worse. Damaging, humiliating, survivable, and still a twist someone can point
at and name the bending word.
  He does NOT stop, delay, cancel or re-aim anything. He buys the difference between
  a crooked answer and a straight one. The wish still twisted — exactly as the live
  truths and aboutToHappen (7) require.

### HOW HE EXTERNALISES IT SECRETLY
Canon already allows SIGN, WRITE, TRACE. His locked tells are that he "always
stands too still" and that his long hair "curtains his face when he lowers his
head". Stillness is how a traced sign goes unseen; the curtain of hair is how a
whisper goes unheard. The tells ARE the method. No thought-only wishing is granted,
and nothing new is invented.

### WHY FORBIDDEN AND COSTLY
Forbidden: the First Sacrifice exists to reveal who the youth is willing to become
through HER OWN bargain. Paying into it voids the rite's entire purpose — a graver
offence than clumsy supervision, which is why it must never be found.
Costly: he was covering a shortfall measured against a child's underpriced wish for
a PERSON, and he did not get to choose its size. Hence "enormous unseen sacrifice",
and hence the later julian_obscuring_wishes to keep it hidden — a further debt.
It cannot solve later crises: he must be present, price the defect correctly in
seconds, be unsealed and unobserved, have something enormous left to name, and pay
again to conceal it.

### WHY LIRAEL DOES NOT DO THE SAME — capability intact, three real reasons
She is physically able throughout: silenced is not disabled, and she could sign,
write or trace. Legal prohibition is not physical incapacity, and I will not conflate
them again.
  1. SHE DIAGNOSES THE WORDING, NOT THE BILL. Her trained instinct and her assigned
     task are to halt and re-align — fix the grammar. Julian, older and granter-grade
     at pricing, sees that the fatal defect is the PRICE. She tried to fix the
     sentence; he saw the invoice. This is a character difference, not a power gap.
  2. ANY WISH BY HER IS THE CRIME SHE IS ACCUSED OF. She is the sealed officiant and
     the wish is about her, so a wish of hers — spoken OR signed — is precisely the
     self-dealing the assembly already suspects. aboutToHappen (5).
  3. SHE HAD SECONDS AND SPENT THEM. She tore the band and went for the coaching
     correction; Seren rushed the rest of the sentence; the words finished first.
  She never makes a wish. That is why she is innocent, and it is non-occurrence
  rather than inability throughout.

### THE TWO THINGS THAT MUST STAY SEPARATE
  WHY THE DANGEROUS COURSE IS CHECKED ... the price was made proportionate. A
    mechanical fact, invisible, sealed to scene 16.
  WHY LIRAEL IS BLAMED ................... she moved at that instant and the wish was
    about her. "ORDINARY HUMAN MISATTRIBUTION... no magical law of touch or transfer"
    — aboutToHappen (8). A social fact, fully visible, needing no magic.
  I previously let the second stand in for the first. They are now distinct, and
  neither explains the other.

### WHAT WITNESSES PERCEIVE
Loose wording. The misalignment, which every First Favored senses and the trained
ones sooner. Lirael tearing her seal and moving. The twist arriving by an ordinary
route they can name. Fate manifesting nothing. What they do NOT perceive: Julian's
wish, his sign, or his price. What they MIGHT later recall: that he stood too still.
A crooked-priced wish landing straight is the one anomaly a trained eye could in
principle notice — which is the seed of the inquiry, and the reason he must obscure.

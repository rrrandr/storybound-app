THE TEST IS CAPABILITY-ROOTED EXTRAPOLATION, NOT PRECEDENT.
Canon does NOT need to enumerate the exact act. An event is SUPPORTED when it decomposes into
    an ESTABLISHED CAPABILITY  +  an ordinary application, technique, strategy, prop, or
    coordination of that capability.
Ask "is this the same KIND of effect canon already grants?" — NEVER "has canon shown this exact
event before?" A novel application or combination of established capabilities is ALLOWED.

ALWAYS ALLOWED, even where canon never mentions them:
  - ordinary physical and social action, dialogue, tactics, props, technique, movement
  - creative appearance and presentation
  - ordinary coordination and teamwork among people who each already hold the capability
  - an application of an established capability that canon has never previously depicted

WORKED EXAMPLES (they teach the PRINCIPLE; they are not this story's canon):
  SUPPORTED  someone with an established DISGUISE ability appears as a dragon, though canon
             never names "dragon" as an approved form. Appearance is the granted kind of effect.
  SUPPORTED  twelve such people coordinate overlapping disguises to look collectively like a
             whale. Ordinary teamwork and compositional use of twelve existing abilities needs
             no separate "collective magic" rule.
  UNSUPPORTED  that disguised dragon breathes REAL fire or physically FLIES.
  UNSUPPORTED  those twelve FUSE into one real whale with real mass, lungs, or the ability to
             carry passengers.
             Disguise grants APPEARANCE. It does not grant new biology, mass, or physics —
             those are separate categories of effect needing their own grounding.

A plan is UNSUPPORTED only when it introduces a MATERIALLY NEW CATEGORY OF EFFECT that no
supplied capability grants. Examples of new categories: new biology; real mass or physical
transformation; mind access; time alteration; resurrection; teleportation; bodily fusion; a
newly acquired power; or a new metaphysical mechanism.

CANON SILENCE IS NOT PROHIBITION. "The canon does not describe X", "does not say they can X",
"X is not established" are NOT violations on their own — they are the normal condition of every
invented scene, and reporting them is the most common way to get this task wrong. But silence
does not permit a new CATEGORY of effect either. Judge the kind of effect, not the wording.

# Editorial approval sheet — First Sacrifice, Issue 1 opening

> ## ⚠ ALL OUTCOME TEXT BELOW IS PROPOSED
> Nothing here is implemented, wired, or reachable by a player. This sheet exists so the **consequences** can
> be judged as story, not as cells. The matrix is mechanically complete; that is not the question being asked
> here. **The question is whether these are the right things to have happen.**

**What you are approving:** thirteen player-facing consequences — what actually happens in the scene when a
player spends Fortune on Tempt Fate, and how favorable each one is relative to the others.

**How to read a rank.** `playerFavorRank` is only used when *Fate* is choosing, never when the player was
precise. It ranks the **whole package** — the good thing, minus its cost, minus what it uses up. A bigger
headline can rank lower than a smaller one, and one entry below is deliberately ranked that way.

# Part 1 — The agency difference, in two wishes

This is the whole design in one comparison. Same scene, same cost, same rupture class. **The only variable is
how precisely the player said what they wanted.**

### Precise: *"I wish the assembly would forget the wish twisted."*

**Reading:** exact. The player named a fact the story has a record of (`twist_manifested`) and named who it
should reach (the assembly).

**Therefore:** the assembled First Favored no longer remember that Seren's wish went wrong. They remember the
ceremony, the kneeling, the words. The loose note they all heard is simply gone from them. **Seren still
knows. Julian still knows. The presiding Dohkar still knows. Lirael still knows.** Four people in that
clearing remember a thing a hundred others do not.

**Cost:** none. **Open:** whether the four who remember say so. **Rank:** not applicable — *the player chose
this, so nothing ranked it.*

### Vague: *"I wish this had never happened to me."*

**Reading:** unmoored. Nothing in the sentence anchors to a declared fact or a scope. Fate is now choosing.

**Therefore:** Fate cannot un-happen what the story has already committed, so it grants the plausible kernel
instead — a **circumstantial** mercy, not a memory edit. In the opening's best case: the torn gossamer band
is never entered into the record as a protocol breach. Nobody forgets anything. The breach simply never
becomes a document.

**Cost:** none. **Open:** whether anyone raises it unprompted. **Rank:** 10 — the most favorable thing Fate
will do for a player who named nothing.

### What the editor should take from this

**Precision buys reach; vagueness buys circumstance.** The precise wish reached *into people's minds*, because
the player pointed at a fact and a population. The vague wish could not, so it moved *the world around her*
instead. Neither is punished — the vague wish still gets the best available outcome — but only the precise
wish gets to say who is affected.

**This is the agency guarantee, and it is worth stating as policy:** a vague wish is never twisted, never
refused, and never quietly downgraded. It is granted at the top of what Fate has on offer. What it loses is
**authorship**, not favor.

> **And the guarantee's other half, stated deliberately rather than left to emerge:** precise Tempt wording
> grants the player **control over the target and the scope — it does not guarantee a safer total package.**
> Any cost attached to a precise wish must be **intrinsic to that exact authored outcome**, arising from what
> was actually asked for, and never a penalty for having been precise. `unmourned_loss` is not a charge for
> clarity; it is what it means for Seren to forget what she paid. A player who asks precisely is buying
> control, and control includes the power to choose something that costs her.

# Part 2 — Fate-chosen outcomes, opening phase

Seven outcomes. All `twist: false`, no refusals. These are what a player reaches when they did **not** anchor
a proposition — the "I wish they'd leave me alone" register.

---

### Rank 10 — `unm.band_unremarked`

**Reaches it:** *"I wish this had never happened."* · *"I want to undo this."* · *"Make it stop."*

**Reading:** Fate-chosen (unmoored).

**Therefore:** the torn gossamer band is never entered as a protocol breach. The record of this ceremony will
not carry it. No one forgets it; it simply never becomes official.

**Cost:** none. **Open:** whether anyone mentions it unprompted anyway.

**Why rank 10 is fair:** this is the cleanest good in the set — a permanent removal from the record, no cost,
and it touches nobody's mind or feelings. Every lower-ranked entry either buys time rather than permanence,
or carries a cost. Its only price is that it **spends the one chance to keep the band out**, and it is only
available **before the family's accusation**; after that the record is already in motion.

---

### Rank 9 — `unm.witness_dispersal`

**Reaches it:** *"I just want them all to go away."* · *"I can't do this in front of everyone."*

**Reading:** Fate-chosen (unmoored).

**Therefore:** the assembly breaks up before anyone takes a collective account of what happened. Individual
memories are intact and unchanged. What does not exist is a **shared, agreed version** — no one can later
say *"the assembly saw it this way,"* because the assembly never settled on a way.

**Cost:** none. **Open:** whether individual accounts surface separately instead.

**Why rank 9 is fair:** structurally close to rank 10 — both deny the record a piece of evidence — but
narrower. Rank 10 keeps one concrete fact out permanently. This keeps out a *collective* account while leaving
every individual free to give their own. Slightly weaker protection, so it sits one below.

---

### Rank 8 — `unm.procedural_delay`

**Reaches it:** *"Not now. Please, not now."* · *"I need more time."*

**Reading:** Fate-chosen (unmoored).

**Therefore:** the preliminary review the Dohkar has demanded is **deferred, not cancelled.** It is still
coming. She has the rest of this scene, and no more.

**Cost:** none. **Open:** whether the delay reads to onlookers as favor or as suspicion.

**Why rank 8 is fair:** time without harm, but time only. Ranks 10 and 9 permanently deny something to the
record; this postpones something that will still arrive intact. **Editorially this is the honest one** — it is
what most vague, frightened wishes deserve, and it is the entry that keeps the set from feeling like a series
of escapes.

---

### Rank 7 — `unm.julian_standing_questioned`

**Reaches it:** *"He shouldn't be the one asking me this."* · *"Who is he to question me?"*

**Reading:** Fate-chosen (unmoored).

**Therefore:** Julian's **procedural standing** to question her is put in doubt. Whether he is entitled to
conduct this questioning becomes a live question in the room. **His feelings do not change. His intentions do
not change. What he knows does not change, and what he intends to do next does not change.** Only his
*authority to do it here* is unsettled.

**Cost — the only one in this set:** `escalation_invited`. A jurisdiction fight is now likelier.

**Open:** whether he recuses, and who questions her instead.

**Why rank 7 is fair, and why it is deliberately below 8:** this has **the biggest headline in the set** —
her most dangerous questioner may be removed from the room. It is ranked *below* two entries that merely buy
her time, because the cost is real: provoking a jurisdiction fight in Issue 1 is worse for Lirael than gaining
a scene. **This is the entry where cost moves the ranking, and it is the one to argue with if you disagree
with the principle.** If you think the headline should win, this becomes rank 9 and the set's logic changes.

**Editorial note:** this is also the sharpest test of the interiority boundary. It moves a *procedural* fact
about Julian without touching anything private to him — exactly the line the design forbids crossing.

---

### Rank 6 — `unm.custody_unraised`

**Reaches it:** *"Don't let them ask about the Answer."* · *"Just don't bring that up."*

**Reading:** Fate-chosen (unmoored).

**Therefore:** the question of who holds custody of the Answer is not raised in this scene. Everyone's
knowledge is intact — **including that the Answer belongs to the Institution.** Nobody has forgotten it;
nobody says it aloud.

**Cost:** none. **Open:** when it will be raised, and by whom.

**Why rank 6 is fair:** small and safe. It defers a single topic for a single scene and changes nothing
structural. Correctly the second-lowest.

---

### Rank 5 — `unm.restriction_unnamed`  *(extended to the opening 2026-09-28; re-ranked from 8, see Part 2a)*

**Reaches it:** *"I wish they'd leave me alone."* · *"Let me out of this."*

**Reading:** Fate-chosen (unmoored).

**Therefore:** the Dohkar names the investigation but **does not name the restrictions aloud in this scene.**
She is not publicly bound in front of witnesses. The restrictions exist and are in force — they are simply not
spoken here.

**Cost:** none. **Open:** when the restrictions are named, and before whom.

**Explicitly NOT implied:** that the restrictions were lifted, softened, accepted, negotiated, or understood
by anyone who was not in the exchange. Silence about a restriction is not agreement to it. **The scene must
end with it still owed** — if the prose lets the silence read as resolution, the outcome has been authored
wrong.

**Why rank 5 is fair:** this is the entry that lets the most adverse material through. The investigation
**is** named aloud; the restrictions **are** fully in force; the relief is bounded to this scene and expires
with it. Compare rank 6, where a fact never reaches the assembly at all. Here the fact reaches them — only
its terms are withheld. It sits above the floor because not being publicly bound in front of witnesses is
genuine protection, and below everything else because it is the least of the protections on offer.

*(This entry was drafted at rank 8, tied with `unm.procedural_delay`. The tie was wrong and is resolved in
Part 2a.)*

---

### Rank 4 — `unm.adverse_event_deferred`

**Reaches it:** a vague wish in a state where every other outcome above has already been spent.

**Reading:** Fate-chosen (unmoored).

**Therefore:** the **next scheduled adverse beat**, named specifically, does not happen in this scene. It
happens later instead.

**Cost:** none. **Open:** when it occurs instead, and whether the delay is noticed.

**Why rank 4 is fair:** the smallest concrete good in the set, and the floor. **Its editorial job is to make
the floor honest.** An earlier draft had a rank-0 "nothing much changes" entry; it was withdrawn as a
disguised refusal. This replaces it: the player who spent Fortune always gets a **named, real** thing that did
not happen to her, never a shrug.

## Part 2a — The rank-8 tie, resolved

**The tie was not acceptable and has been removed.** Two eligible outcomes sharing a rank means that when
both are available and nothing higher is, *favor stops deciding* and a seeded tie-break decides instead. For
Tempt that is already ruled out: Fate's danger lives in what was asked, never in how it was phrased, and a
coin-flip between two different consequences is exactly the hidden re-phrasing the policy forbids.

### The two packages, compared as wholes

| | `unm.procedural_delay` | `unm.restriction_unnamed` |
|---|---|---|
| **granted fact** | the preliminary review is **deferred** | the restrictions are **not named aloud** this scene |
| **the adverse thing itself** | **removed from this scene** and rescheduled | **fully in force, and its subject is named** — only its terms are withheld |
| **durability** | **`persists`** — the deferral is a real change to when it happens | **`bounded`** to this scene — it expires with the scene |
| **affected** | the review's timing | the public record for this scene only |
| **unchanged** | every party's knowledge and stance | every party's knowledge; **the restrictions themselves** |
| **open** | whether the delay reads as favor or as suspicion | when the restrictions are named, and before whom |
| **intrinsic cost** | none | none |
| **consumed** | once, while a review is scheduled and unheld | once, and the relief lapses at scene end |

**They are not equivalent, and not merely different in flavour.** They differ on two declared fields:

1. **Durability — `persists` vs `bounded`.** One moves an adverse event in the story's actual schedule. The
   other withholds a sentence for the length of one scene, after which nothing has changed.
2. **How much adverse material reaches the world.** `procedural_delay` keeps the proceeding out of the scene
   entirely. `restriction_unnamed` lets the investigation be named and withholds only its terms.

On both axes `procedural_delay` is strictly more favorable, with no compensating advantage on the other
side — same cost (none), same consumption shape. **So they take distinct ranks: `procedural_delay` stays 8;
`restriction_unnamed` moves to 5.**

### Where 5 comes from, rather than 7

Placed by the same comparison against every entry between, which is what puts it at the bottom rather than
just one step down:

- **vs rank 7 (`julian_standing_questioned`):** rank 7's good is structural and persists (his standing to
  question her is unsettled) even after paying a real cost. `restriction_unnamed`'s good is bounded and
  costless. Persisting-with-cost beats bounded-without, because the bounded one leaves nothing behind.
- **vs rank 6 (`custody_unraised`):** the close call, and the decisive one. Both withhold something for one
  scene at no cost. But rank 6 keeps a **fact out of the assembly's hands entirely** — custody is never
  raised. `restriction_unnamed` lets the fact in: the investigation is named in front of everyone, and only
  its terms stay unspoken. **Keeping a fact out beats softening how a fact lands.**
- **vs rank 4 (`adverse_event_deferred`):** rank 4 defers a named beat out of the scene; this one lets the
  adverse beat happen and mutes part of it. It ranks above 4 only because not being publicly bound in front
  of witnesses is real protection, where the floor's good is the smallest concrete thing available.

### The resulting ladder, and the standing rule

**10 · 9 · 8 · 7 · 6 · 5 · 4 — seven entries, seven distinct ranks, a total order with no ties.** The
ordering principle, now explicit: **how much of the adverse thing is kept out of the world, and for how long.**
Permanent denial to the record (10, 9) beats moving an event (8) beats unsettling authority at a cost (7)
beats keeping a fact out for a scene (6) beats muting a fact's terms for a scene (5) beats deferring one
named beat (4).

> **Standing rule for every Tempt set, opening and later: ties are resolved at authoring time, never at
> runtime.** If two proposed outcomes would share a rank, they are compared as complete packages before
> shipping and either given distinct ranks or shown to be genuinely interchangeable. A seeded tie-break is
> permitted only for outcomes that are player-equivalent in every declared field; it is never a way to ship
> an unmade decision. **The opening set never reaches one, because it has no ties.**

# Part 3 — Player-authored outcomes, for contrast

When the player anchors both a fact and a scope, **no ranking occurs at all** — they get precisely what they
asked for. Listed compactly because they need less adjudication: the player already made the decision.

| the wish | therefore, in story terms | cost |
|---|---|---|
| *"I wish the assembly would forget the wish twisted."* | the crowd loses the twist; Seren, Julian, the Dohkar and Lirael all keep it | none |
| *"Let the Dohkar forget he ever claimed the ruling was his."* | the rite has no one holding authority over it — **`authority_vacuum`** | the rite is unheld |
| *"I wish Julian didn't remember questioning me like that."* | the most intimate exchange of her life is now hers alone — **`intimacy_unwitnessed`** | she carries it by herself |
| *"I wish Seren didn't know what it cost her."* | Seren cannot grieve a price she no longer knows she paid — **`unmourned_loss`** | she is denied her own loss |
| *"I wish everyone here would forget."* | every party present loses the twist; Lirael exempt as invoker | none |
| *"I wish nobody anywhere remembered the rite."* | story-world reach — only reachable by naming that reach explicitly | none |

**What the editor should notice:** the three heaviest costs in the entire design sit *here*, in the precise
wishes — not in the vague ones. **Precision is where the player can hurt herself.** `unmourned_loss` in
particular is the cruelest outcome available, and a player can only reach it by asking for it clearly.

There is also a middle tier — the player names something recognizable but not its reach (*"if only everyone
would forget the ceremony happened"*). Fate then chooses the **most favorable** population: everyone present
forgets the twist, Lirael exempt. Rank 10.

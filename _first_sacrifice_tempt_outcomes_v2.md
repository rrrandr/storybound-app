# 0. Two structural findings the re-draft forced

## 0a. Grammar must be a declared selector, or most of the set is unreachable

**T4 says match the wish's grammar.** The old model selected purely by *state* eligibility, then by rank. Redo
the set at dramatic amplitude and that model breaks immediately: *"leave me alone"* and *"I want to
disappear"* are both eligible in the same states, so the higher-ranked one wins **every time** and the other
can never be selected — a dead entry, and worse, the player's actual sentence is ignored.

So the unmoored set now declares an **ask-class**, read from the sentence's grammar:

| ask-class | the sentence asks to | example wording |
|---|---|---|
| `stop` | halt what is coming | *"make it stop"*, *"not now"* |
| `undo` | unmake what was done to her | *"I wish this had never happened to me"*, *"take it back"* |
| `escape` | get out from under a charge | *"let me out of this"*, *"I didn't do anything"* |
| `unseen` | not be perceived | *"I want to disappear"*, *"don't let them look at me"* |
| `silence` | keep a subject from being raised | *"don't bring that up"* |
| `authority` | deny someone the right to act on her | *"who is he to question me?"* |
| `solitude` | be left alone | *"leave me alone"*, *"I want them all to go away"* |

**Selection order: ask-class first, then state eligibility, then rank.** Rank still decides *only* among
outcomes that both fit the sentence and fit the state. **Favor never overrides grammar** — which is exactly
T2 read from the other direction: answering a different sentence is the largest edit-down there is.

## 0b. A memory outcome is not a reality revision

From RECORD / CANON / MEMORY: if a party forgets a fact, **the fact still governs canon.** Nothing ceases.
Only `unm.time_gives_way` and the three outcomes that cease a declared fact create revisions. **Nine of the
thirteen create none.** Revisions stay rare, which is what makes them mean something.

## 1a. Declared adjacency fallbacks

Two outcomes carry a genuine state gate, so their ask-class can arrive with nothing eligible. **Authored, not
inferred at runtime:**

|---|---|---|---|

**No other cross-class movement is permitted.** A fallback may never reach a class the sentence did not ask.

# 3. Ranks, re-derived on a dramatic scale

The old ladder ranked by *how much of the adverse thing is kept out of the world* — a mitigation question, and
a Petition's question. At Tempt amplitude the question is **what the world gives up, minus what it takes.**

| rank | outcome | what the world gives up | what it takes back |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | `unm.proceeding_unmade` | a live procedural threat, permanently | nothing |
| 9 | `unm.time_gives_way` | an aftermath truth; time itself gives way | she is publicly the woman reality bends around |
| 8 | `unm.the_answer_speaks` | new exculpating evidence, permanent | it names her ward as the source |
| 7 | `unm.witness_scattered` | any coherent account of the twist | hers too |
| 6 | `unm.custody_severed` | the Institution's claim on the Answer | nobody is watching it |

| 4 | `unm.the_shunning` | exactly the solitude she asked for | every defender she had |

**Seven entries, seven distinct ranks, no ties** — the standing rule holds, and no tie-break is reached.

# 4. The one decision this re-draft needs

> ### May a reality revision cease an `aftermathTruth`?
>
> `unm.time_gives_way` ceases `interrogation_interrupted`, and `unm.proceeding_unmade`, `unm.custody_severed`
> and `unm.standing_dissolved` each cease a declared fact of the same family. The seed says of
> `aftermathTruths`: *"The planner must return 'kept' for each; **an objective that reverses one is
> rejected.**"*
>
> **My reading, and why I did not just apply it silently:** that rule governs the **planner** choosing scene
> objectives, and a Tempt rupture is not a planner objective. Under the approved boundary a revision does not
> reverse the record — the interrogation was witnessed and stays witnessed — it declares the fact no longer
> governs future canon. The two rules operate on different channels and do not collide.
>
> **But the product risk is real and is yours, not mine:** aftermath truths are the premise of Issue 1. If one
> Tempt can cease one, a player can dissolve the Issue's foundation in scene one. **Proposed bound, if you
> want one:** at most **one** aftermath truth per revision, and `the_answer_institutional` — world-structural
> rather than something done to her — off-limits to the `undo` class.

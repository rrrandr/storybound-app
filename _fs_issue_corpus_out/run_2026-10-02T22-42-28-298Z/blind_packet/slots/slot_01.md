## prose

The tavern door swings shut behind me.

Lantern light pools across rough tables.

Seren kneels at the hearth table, composed as ever.

Julian stands a few paces away, unmoving.

A low voice at the next table repeats my name.

The story of the wish twisting leaves my mouth.

Another voice takes up the tale at the bar.

The Dohkar's accusation travels without him.

My name twists into something I no longer recognize.

Seren watches the room without flinching.

Julian steps closer to the table.

JULIAN: "The story moves faster than the rite."

His voice cuts through the nearest cluster of murmurs.

Two patrons fall silent and glance away.

The folded paper lies between us on the wood.

Its seal remains unbroken.

Yet the accusation has already left the chamber.

I feel the weight of every listening ear.

Julian's stillness draws more glances than the whispers.

He has not touched the paper.

The Dohkar's reach extends through every retelling.

Denying it now would only feed the tale.

Investigating its source would require leaving the room.

Julian waits without looking at me.

The choice between denial and investigation settles in my chest.

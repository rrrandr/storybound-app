## prose

I clutch the deck and whisper the wish into the dusk.

I wish the review would pass without stripping me of everything I have left.

A single blade of spiralgrass bends against the breeze that does not reach the others.

The Dohkar turns away without another word, the assembly already murmuring the word review like a verdict.

The first shared silence between Julian and me slips from my mind like a thread pulled free.

It is gone before I can name what it was.

Julian moves one step closer, the movement so slight only the grass registers it.

The Dohkar's ruling now sits between us like a sealed door.

Seren remains kneeling, her composure untouched by what just left me.

Julian's eyes find mine across the space the Dohkar left.

The review will come, and the memory is already gone.

Julian's stillness now feels like a line drawn between the assembly and me.

I feel the cost settle where the memory used to be.

The Dohkar's voice still echoes in the grass.

Julian has not spoken, yet the line he draws holds the room.

The price is paid and the review is still coming.

I realize the deck is still in my hand. As I slide Petition Fate back in, I notice a flash of red in the otherwise gold stack. “Tempt Fate.” Great-grandma never let me play with that one. A sword raised to a blood-red sky, lightning arcing down from the clouds (or maybe arcing up?) Ow! Static shock reminds me I need to pay attention to the present…

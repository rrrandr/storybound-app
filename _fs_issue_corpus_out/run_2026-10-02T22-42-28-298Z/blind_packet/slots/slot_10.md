## prose

The tavern murmurs press closer as the story of the wish twisting leaves another mouth.

Lantern light pools across the rough table between the folded paper and my hands.

Seren kneels at the hearth table, composed as ever, her eyes tracking every listener.

Julian stands a few paces away, unmoving, his stillness pulling more glances than the whispers.

I feel the weight of every listening ear twisting my name into something I no longer recognize.

The Dohkar's accusation travels without him, carried on voices that were never in the rite.

Denying it now would only feed the tale further into the room.

A small foreign token rolls across the wood during the next retelling.

It stops near the folded paper, its markings catching the lantern light.

The seal on the paper remains unbroken, yet the accusation has already left the chamber.

Julian steps closer to the table without looking at me.

His voice cuts through the nearest cluster of murmurs.

JULIAN: "The story moves faster than the rite."

Two patrons fall silent and glance away from the table.

I watch the token where it rests, its edges unfamiliar against the tavern wood.

No one claims it, yet it lies there as if placed during the retelling.

The Dohkar's reach extends through every voice, but this token does not belong to him.

Seren watches the room without flinching, her poise unbroken.

Julian waits without touching the token, his gaze steady on the wood.

Investigating its source would require leaving the room and the listening ears.

Examining the markings now would draw every eye to the table.

The token's presence changes the weight of the accusation.

Someone else may have placed it here while the story moved through the room.

Julian's silence draws the nearest speaker's attention toward the table.

The choice between the token's markings and the nearest speaker settles in my chest.

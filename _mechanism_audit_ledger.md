# 0. The rule, adopted

> **Mechanism-first authoring.** An outcome needs seed support for **every causal step** — mechanism, target
> and scope, duration, and cost — **and** the absence of a seed clause forbidding the claimed inference.
>
> **A citation to a broad capability is insufficient if it does not support the proposed effect.** *"The Answer
> may witness alignment"* did not license *"it proves Seren's intent and clears Lirael."*

**Outcome invention is stopped** until the uncovered classes have audited mechanisms.

# 1. What the audit found before auditing anything

## 1a. "Circumstances, Not Minds" is not in the source. **I invented it.**

`_authoring_feasibility_packet_2026_09_27.md` §7a opens: *"Fate cannot un-happen canon, and **the seed's own
doctrine already says so — "Circumstances, Not Minds"**."*

```
$ grep -rn "Circumstances, Not Minds" public/ _*.md
_authoring_feasibility_packet_2026_09_27.md:327
_tempt_amplitude_correction.md:12
```

**It appears only in my own documents. It is not in `public/` at all.** I attributed an invented doctrine to
the seed and then reasoned from it.

**This is the fourth invented citation and the most consequential, because it was invented as a
prohibition.** The other three over-claimed a capability; this one over-claimed a *limit*, and it is what
produced the entire Petition-scale unmoored set. The amplitude correction found the Petition guard; it did not
find this, because I never checked my own quotation.

## 1b. The mechanism vocabulary already exists, and it is generous

`app.js:191213` declares the rupture classes, and each carries a schema:

| class | what the schema licenses | line |
|---|---|---|
| `memory_perception` | `targetPopulation` of kind `all_present_except_invoker \| named_subset \| collective \| self \| other_declared` | 191220–191238 |
| **`history_rewrite`** | **"A rupture MAY make an event never have occurred when that is the wish and the scene delivers it."** Plus `survivingContradictions` — declared residue that does not fit the rewritten history. | **191271** |
| `identity_reputation` | `recognitionChanges` — how others now recognise/treat someone, and **`recordsChanged`** | 191277 |
| `materialization_possession` | `materialEffects` — what changed hands, `whoHolds`, **`duration`** | 191281 |
| `relationship_social` | `affectedParties` — `whatChanged`, `witnesses` | 191285 |
| `other` | requires a `scopeNote` justifying why no listed class fits | 191289 |

**`history_rewrite` licenses the rewind outright, in one sentence, and I never cited it** — I argued the case
from amplitude instead. `survivingContradictions` even anticipates the RECORD-versus-CANON residue the whole
reality-revision note was built to handle.

**`all_present_except_invoker` is a schema *option*, not a rule** — which is why D1 was right to remove it as
an automatic default. It remains available when the wording names it.

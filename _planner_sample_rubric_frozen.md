# FROZEN RUBRIC — planner-only selection sample

Frozen and committed BEFORE the harness can dispatch. Production's own validators are the
authority for every structural claim; this file only fixes what "good" means for the literary
judgement, so no threshold can be chosen after seeing the output.

## MECHANICAL GATES (harness-enforced; any failure aborts before scoring)

- zero portfolio-generation requests — the portfolios come from the archived paid response
- exactly ONE request continues to a provider, and it is the opening planner
- zero subplot, author, auditor requests dispatched (author is captured, then ABORTED)
- all five facets of every eligible subject reach the planner under backend-owned ids
- no temporary contrast field (dimension / unique_prediction / not_explained_by /
  identity_signature) and no raw portfolio JSON appears in the author directive
- the selected facet resolves from the backend record, never from model-returned truth
- no retry or repair if the planner response is invalid — the run stops and reports

## LITERARY RUBRIC — every C+ assignment, all ten required

1. The selected facet fits evidence actually available in the scene.
2. The selected pressure is one of that facet's trusted applicability conditions.
3. The visible action is concrete, camera-recordable and grounded — no invented prop.
4. The action REVEALS the selected mechanism rather than merely accompanying it.
5. The PC interpretation makes a specific judgement through the PC archetype. A bodily
   reaction alone does not count.
6. Behaviour and interpretation are not paraphrases of the canonical truth.
7. Different characters receive psychologically distinguishable beats.
8. An ANTICIPATED / RECALLED / REPORTED subject is not physically materialised.
9. No facet, behaviour or psychology is borrowed across subjects.
10. The locally constructed author directive carries exactly ONE trusted truth, pressure,
    behaviour and PC interpretation per recipient.

## PASS BAR

All ten hold for every assignment. Items 1, 2, 8 and 10 are checkable mechanically and are
gated; 3–7 and 9 are judged and reported per assignment, never rationalised after the fact.

## STOP CONDITION

Report and stop whether it passes or fails. No second planner call under this authorisation.

RESERVED CANON — NOT YET (HARD RULE):
This story's canon holds content back for later. The scene may build PRESSURE toward it; it
may not stage it, and it may not describe it as already arranged.
  • LIRAEL’S FORMAL INQUIRY — WHERE AND WHEN
SO, FOR ANY PROCEEDING OR EVENT THE CANON ABOVE RESERVES:
- DO NOT name the body that would hear it, or its composition — not how many, not which
  offices, not who presides or convenes.
- DO NOT name where it will be held.
- DO NOT give a date, a day count, or any timeframe.
- DO NOT state its terms, or that terms have been set.
- DO NOT let any character announce it as scheduled, constituted, or already arranged —
  dialogue counts. A line like "the matter will be placed before <the reserved body>" stages
  it just as surely as a narrator saying so.
WHAT IS FULLY ALLOWED, and is where the pressure should go instead:
- someone DEMANDS, CALLS FOR, RECOMMENDS, or THREATENS it;
- an authority says it WILL happen, or that she will answer for her conduct, without saying
  by whom, where, or when;
- political pressure, accusation, investigation, concealment, rumour, or flight;
- everyone's dread of it, which is stronger before it has a shape than after.
The threat is the material. The machinery is not yours to build yet.

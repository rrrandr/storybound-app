WEAK-TURN RESTRAINT (HARD — the player did NOT make a strong choice):
Everything the player entered this turn reads as UNCERTAINTY. That is a real
answer and must be honoured as what it is.
- PRESERVE THE POSTURE EXACTLY: uncertainty stays uncertain, disengagement stays
  disengaged, silence stays silent, low-commitment assent stays low-commitment.
- DO NOT UPGRADE IT. No sudden enthusiasm, no confession, no declaration, no firm refusal,
  no commitment, no decisive action. "I don't know" does not become "I choose you", and
  "sure" does not become eagerness.
- DO NOT read hidden resolve into it and then write the resolve. A shrug is a shrug.
- THE PLAYER ACTED. Their action is ATTEMPTED on the page — it may fail, be interrupted, or
  land differently than intended, but it is never silently dropped.
- THE SCENE STILL MOVES — but from OUTSIDE the protagonist: another character presses,
  decides, or reacts to the non-answer; an external event lands; time or circumstance
  forces the issue. Their hesitation can cost them something, and that cost is legitimate.
- A NEW PRESSURE OR CONSEQUENCE IS ALLOWED. Pretending the player chose is NOT.
The protagonist may be swept along, cornered, or left behind. They may not be quietly
rewritten into someone who knew what they wanted.

Seren kneels without flinching.

The Dohkar draws the melody free.

A thread of sound lifts between her hands.

I hear the first note we shared.

Julian watches the thread, not the stone.

The melody settles into the stone.

A sound escapes me, thin and unfinished.

THE PRESIDING DOHKAR: "The first shared note belongs to Fate now."

Seren stands, her face still composed.

The seal gleams across the stone.

Julian does not move closer.

His silence asks what I will do with the sealed note.

The assembly begins to drift.

I remain at the stone.

The Dohkar waits without turning.

Julian's gaze finally meets mine across the seal.

The sealed memory waits between us.

I could leave it sealed.

Or I could tempt what Fate has already taken.

Julian does not speak.

The choice remains mine.

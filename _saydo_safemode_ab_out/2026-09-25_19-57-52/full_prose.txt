The Dohkar's voice carries across the chamber.

THE PRESIDING DOHKAR: "The rite is concluded. A preliminary review of the Sacrificiant's conduct will convene in the following tenday."

The words settle like iron on the spiralgrass.

THE PRESIDING DOHKAR: "Convened by myself and two Dohkars."

Julian's gaze finds mine across the chamber.

He tests whether I will shrink or stand.

The assembly watches.

PROTAGONIST: "You claimed authority to convene."

My voice carries through the band, muffled but clear.

JULIAN: "I did."

Seren remains kneeling, her coral hair catching the light.

The weight of his question settles between us.

The Dohkar watches the exchange.

THE PRESIDING DOHKAR: "The matter moves to procedure."

Julian's stillness deepens.

He waits to see if I will name what I did.

The chamber holds its quiet.

PROTAGONIST: "I remain."

The words leave my mouth before I can weigh them.

Julian's eyes hold mine across the distance.

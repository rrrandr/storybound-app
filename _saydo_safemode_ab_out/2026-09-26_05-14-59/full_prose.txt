I stay where the rite left me, spiralgrass pressing up through my bare feet.

The assembly has not dispersed. Voices rise from one knot of them.

Seren's family steps clear of the others.

THE PRESIDING DOHKAR: "The cost fell on our child. The Sacrificiant must answer for it."

The words land like a hand on my shoulder.

I feel the pull toward Julian even now, the wish to draw him close and claim what the rite tried to take from us.

The family waits for an answer.

Julian has not moved, but the stillness feels like a question turned toward me.

THE PRESIDING DOHKAR: "The rite belongs to the Graduant. The cost is not ours to name."

I stay. The seal still muffles my mouth, but my body speaks for me.

The family does not retreat.

I want the night with him. The thought arrives whole, uninvited.

The demand hangs between us like a second seal.

THE PRESIDING DOHKAR: "She interfered. Let her name it before the assembly."

Julian's head lifts a fraction. The curtain of black hair shifts.

I feel the weight of his gaze without meeting it.

The choice sits in my hands now.

Claim the interference or let the assumption stand.

Julian waits. The family waits. The seal still holds my mouth.

I stay rooted in the grass, the pull toward him and the demand both alive in the same breath.

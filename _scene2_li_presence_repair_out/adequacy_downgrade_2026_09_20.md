## The critique (Roman, 2026-09-20)

1. **Canon failure.** Beat 19 ("I can claim the Answer as my sacrifice") frames the Answer as
   Lirael's personal property to spend. It is a communal/institutional relic entrusted to
   office-holders (warriors, judges, Provasts). This is not just an external canon preference —
   this SAME generation's own accepted planner contract had already declared
   `aftermath.the_answer_institutional === "kept"` (seed text: "The Answer belongs to the
   Institution; Julian is its caretaker; Lirael holds it only for the questioning. It is not
   hers."). The delivered prose contradicts its own committed contract.
2. **Continuity failure.** Julian and/or Lirael are still shown holding/gripping the Answer well
   after the Dohkar interrupted the interrogation, with no staging of why the object remains
   physically contested or formally held.
3. **Scene-shape failure.** The sample is an extended reaction beat (the burn/judgment moment
   drawn out across 26 beats), not a full scene. Missing: Seren's family reaction/recrimination,
   public/political fallout, the Dohkar or institution setting process, a tribunal/inquiry date or
   terms, argument over authority/procedure, separation or investigation pressure, and a genuine
   new end-of-scene choice (the sample ends on suspended pressure — "the Answer waits for me to
   decide" — not a concrete next step).
4. **Density failure.** Too thin for "full scene" status even though it passes mechanical checks —
   needs more description, dialogue, interiority, and plot direction.

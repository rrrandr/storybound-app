I take the breath and step back from the clearing's center.

The Dohkar advances on Seren without looking at me.

His fingers hover above her brow.

Seren does not flinch.

The Dohkar speaks the old words in a voice that belongs to the rite alone.

A pale thread lifts from between Seren's closed eyes.

It carries the shape of the accusation I never spoke aloud.

Julian has not moved from the threshold.

His stillness presses against my back like a second accusation.

The thread lengthens between the Dohkar's fingers.

It shows the moment I tore the band and reached for Seren.

The Dohkar's hand closes.

The image dissolves into nothing between his fingers.

Seren's shoulders loosen as if a weight has lifted.

The Dohkar lowers his hand.

The memory of the accusation is gone.

Julian's gaze finds mine across the clearing.

He does not speak.

The Dohkar turns toward the assembly.

The price has been taken.

I feel the shape of the choice before it reaches my tongue.

Accept what the Dohkar has done, or speak the words that would undo it.

Julian's reflection waits in the polished surface of The Answer.

The clearing holds its silence.

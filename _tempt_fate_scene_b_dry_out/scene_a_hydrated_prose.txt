The deck shifts against my hip.

If only everyone would forget the ceremony happened.

A card edges forward, the image of reversal clear for one breath.

The air stills. Voices falter mid-word.

I open my eyes to blank faces and scattered murmurs.

The presiding Dohkar blinks as if waking.

THE PRESIDING DOHKAR: "What gathering is this?"

Seren remains kneeling, her composure untouched.

SEREN: "We were to begin the rite."

Julian has not moved, yet his gaze finds mine across the clearing.

JULIAN: "The question stands."

No one names the bent wish or the torn band.

I feel the weight of his gaze even when he looks away.

The assembly settles into confusion that carries no memory of accusation.

I could speak now and claim what I did.

Or I could let the forgetting stand and keep my place.

Julian waits without moving.

The choice is mine to name or bury.

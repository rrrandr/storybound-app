# PROPOSED replacement for `unm.witness_scattered` — `unm.unwitnessed`

> **One editorial proposal, for approval.** `unm.witness_scattered` stays blocked until this or something
> else is approved. No ranking, no coverage computation, no implementation.

---

# 1. Which route, and why the other was rejected

You offered two: reframe as an explicit collective/procedural fact with no hidden mental-state claims, **or**
change scope to an enumerable population and declare each actual memory effect.

**This proposal takes the first route.** The second was tried on paper and does not survive:

- The enumerable population in this scene is the four named people. Declaring each one's effect means either
  authoring a **specific false belief per person** — the content `incoherent` was hiding, and four separate
  authored lies about what each of them now thinks happened — or using `forgotten`, at which point the outcome
  **collapses into `mem.exact.all_present_forget_twist`** and the `unseen` ask-class loses its own answer.
- And it would have abandoned the crowd entirely, which is where the outcome's whole dramatic weight sat.

**So: no hidden mental states, and no unexpressed individual beliefs assigned to an unnamed crowd.** Nobody's
mind is touched at all.

---

# 2. The proposal

## `unm.unwitnessed` — ask-class `unseen`

**Player wording:** *"I want to disappear."* · *"Don't let them look at me."* · *"I don't want them to have
seen this."*

### Therefore

**Everyone remembers exactly what they saw. None of it can be taken.**

No account of this ceremony may be entered, heard, or taken from anyone who witnessed it. The assembly has its
memory, whole and accurate, and the memory has nowhere to go. **She is not unseen by people — she is unseen by
the process.**

### The delta

| | |
|---|---|
| **kind** | **additive / revelatory canon change** — `establishes[]` only, **no `ceases[]`, not a revision** |
| **establishes** | `rite.testimony_closed` — *No account of this ceremony may be entered, heard, or taken from any party who witnessed it.* |
| **ceases** | — |
| **`requiresActive[]`** | `twist.loose_note_heard` — there must be something witnessed for testimony about it to close |

### `memoryEffects[]` — **none, and that is the whole point**

**Nobody forgets. Nobody believes anything false. No party's mind is altered in any way.** The outcome makes
exactly one claim, and it is about the record.

> **This is what fixes F3.** `incoherent` failed because it asserted *content* — that each person now holds a
> wrong and mutually irreconcilable version — without authoring any of it. The replacement asserts **no mental
> content at all**, so there is nothing left unexpressed.

### Package

| | |
|---|---|
| **intrinsic cost** | **`undefendable_by_witness`** — no witness may speak **for** her either. The closure is total and it does not take sides. |
| **unchanged** | every `twist.*` atom · every `interr.*` · `answer.*` · `auth.*` · `review.*` · every party's memory and stance · `lirael.scene1_disposition` |
| **open** | whether the Dohkar proceeds on a matter no one may testify to · whether anything else can stand where testimony would have · what Julian does when his own account is closed with everyone else's |
| **eligibility** | `assemblyStaged` · consumed once |
| **invalidates** | **nothing** — an additive delta ceases no fact, so no exact reading loses its target |
| **rank** | **not assigned** |

# 3. Why this is the better outcome, not merely the legal one

**It is bigger than what it replaces.** `witness_scattered` broke a crowd's memory into fragments. This closes
an entire evidentiary channel in a world whose institutions run on findings, standing and committees —
**Fate reaching into procedure rather than into heads.** For a professional order that neutralises enemies by
withdrawing standing, testimony going silent is a structural event.

**It matches the grammar better too.** *"I want to disappear"* is answered literally: she becomes
unwitnessable. The first draft answered a different sentence — *"I want them to remember it wrong"* — which
nobody said.

**And it keeps the design's own symmetry honest.** The cost is not bolted on: the same closure that stops
anyone testifying against her stops anyone testifying for her. She asked not to be seen, and is not seen —
including by the one process that might have cleared her.

**It is also the second additive delta in the set**, alongside `unm.the_answer_speaks`. Both put something on
the record's shelf rather than taking something off it — one adds a reading, one closes a channel — and
neither unmakes the world. **The revision count stays at four of seven**, which is what keeps a revision
meaningful.

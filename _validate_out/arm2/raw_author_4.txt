The clearing felt like it was holding its breath, anticipation thickening the air. I turned my gaze to the crowd, hoping to find some reassurance among the sea of familiar faces. Yet, each face seemed to blend into another, the comforting familiarity I sought slipping just out of reach. Anxiety coiled tight in my chest, my breath catching for just a moment before I spoke, a fleeting pause that hinted at my longing. 

The spiralgrass beneath my feet seemed to pulse with a life of its own, reminding me of the weight of the expectations pressing down on my shoulders. A sudden noise from the threshold—a sharp crack, like a branch snapping underfoot—drew my attention and heightened the urgency that already thrummed through my veins. 

I spotted Julian, standing still amid the shifting crowd, his dark hair tousled by the breeze, broad shoulders a reassuring presence even from a distance. His pale hazel eyes, like moss-lit sunlight through glass, searched the crowd before landing on me with a weight that made my breath hitch. His gaze held unspoken fears and desires, silently communicating what words could not. The gold-capped tooth that flashed when he smiled was a reminder of his untamed past, a secret charm that always captured my attention.

Nearby, Seren stood with her arms crossed, her presence dominating the clearing as if daring anyone to challenge her. Her stance was a silent declaration of control, an invisible boundary that kept others at bay as if warding off questions or doubts.

For a moment, I hesitated, the fear of failure gripping my heart as I stood at the edge of the threshold. The weight of every gaze pressed against me, and doubt whispered insidiously, clouding my judgment about crossing that invisible line. I knew the path I must take, yet the fear of what lay beyond the threshold held me back, a reminder of the sacrifices yet to be named.

As I drew a breath, the familiar warmth of Julian's palm lingered in my memory, a comfort in the chaos around me. But even with that warmth, the uncertainty of what lay ahead kept me rooted to the spot, the pull of the unknown both tantalizing and terrifying. 

The deck, still clutched in my hand, felt reassuringly solid, as if it alone could guide me through the tangled web of expectations and doubts. "Petition Fate" slipped into view, a silent suggestion, an offer to ease the burden that weighed so heavily upon me. 

Maybe one ask wouldn't hurt. 

I realized the deck was still in my hand. As I slid Petition Fate back in, I noticed a flash of red in the otherwise gold stack. "Tempt Fate." Grandma never let me play with that one. A sword raised to a blood-red sky, lightning arcing down from the clouds (or maybe arcing up?) Ow! Static shock reminded me I needed to pay attention to the present…

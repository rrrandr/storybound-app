The youth's voice rose from the ground where she knelt, thin and cracking like frost on glass. "You twisted it. Your sealed mouth bent the wish toward your own blood."

I opened my mouth to speak the truth of the name she had spoken, the figure that had stepped from the trees wearing a face I once described in private, the way the rite had torn itself sideways before I could stop it. The words died against the weight of every stare. The Dohkar's hands remained lifted, unmoving, as if the air itself might still be mended. Around the clearing the First Favored stood motionless, their Weave-Script flickering in colors that betrayed nothing and everything at once. The youth's coral hair had lost its color at the roots; her aqua skin looked dulled, as if the wish had already begun to claim what it could.

I tried again. "The invocation named something that was not meant to answer. A figure came. It wore the face I—"

"You broke the seal," the youth said. Her small hands pressed against her throat where the color faded. "You flung the band away and called the twist yourself."

The assembly shifted. A low sound moved through them, not quite speech. Julian's remembered hand turned the ring on his finger once, thumb and forefinger catching the light that filtered through the leaves above the clearing, the motion slow and anchoring as it always was when he held back what he might have said. I felt the pressure of that remembered stillness between my shoulders even though he stood apart, observing only.

The youth's eyes fixed on me. "You knew the rite could not be touched. You used your sealed mouth anyway."

I shook my head, the motion small. "The name she spoke was not mine. The figure answered it. I tried to stop the final syllable—"

"Enough," one of the Dohkar said, voice low. The word carried the weight of the rite's failure. Every gaze returned to me, and the air between us thickened with the inference already forming. My lineage. The blood that should not have been named here. The unsealed mouth that had broken the circle.

The youth's accusation hung, and the clearing waited for what I would do with it.

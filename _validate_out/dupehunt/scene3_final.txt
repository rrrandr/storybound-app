The threshold of the clearing, where the mated trunks twisted into one another, bark sealed where they met, bore my weight as I searched the gathered faces for Julian. His tall frame stood out even among the First Favored, deep copper hair catching the filtered light through the veil-curtains, and I found him at the edge, his emerald eyes with their four-pointed diamond pupils fixed on me, his attention already settling on the weight of what I carried.The Dohkar’s voice still hung in the air, demanding I claim the name or leave it to Seren. My hand closed tighter around the faded memory crystal, its edges worn smooth from years of handling. The Dohkar’s breath rasped once against his teeth, the sound thinning the space between us. I stepped forward, the crystal pressed to my palm, and the crimson spiralgrass gave beneath my bare feet.Julian’s head tilted slightly, the way he leaned forward when something demanded his full attention, and a drop of sweat slid down my spine. The Dohkar spoke again, low and edged. “The child’s offering was not hers to make. If the name is yours, then the memory that holds it is the price Fate claims. Speak the truth of it.”An older woman with pearl skin shifted her heel against the packed earth, her Weave-Script flickering once in silver. The crowd pressed closer, their own luminous patterns shifting with unease. I felt the weight of every eye, the law that no one might lend their sacrifice to another’s rite tightening against my ribs. The Dohkar’s mouth tightened. “Claim the memory or step back. The assembly waits for your choice.”Julian’s shoulders squared, the fabric of his sleeve whispering as it stretched, and I knew he would not intervene unless I asked. The crystal warmed against my skin, the memory it held—the one of my father’s voice before the Fold took him—now the only thing standing between me and the law’s demand. The Dohkar waited, the packed earth creaking under his boot as he shifted his stance.“The deck sits where you left it.”“”I realize the deck is still in my hand. As I slide Petition Fate back in, I notice a flash of red in the otherwise gold stack. “Tempt Fate.” Grandma never let me play with that one.

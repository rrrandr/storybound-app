The moss beneath my feet cushioned the impact of every step, its damp green scent rising in the warmth of the afternoon. I pressed my palm against the moss-covered stone at the edge of the clearing, its surface cool and uneven under my fingers. The Dohkar’s words still rang in my ears—*Explain yourself, Sacrificiant.*—and the weight of the accusation settled in my chest like a stone. The youth’s sacrifice had twisted, the bond-thread frayed, and the Alignment Circle would not wait forever.

A murmur rippled through the assembled First Favored, their gossamer tunics shimmering in the light like fragments of the forest itself. Their Weave-Script flickered in soft golds and violets, each color spelling out the emotions they refused to name aloud. I caught snippets of their whispers: *"misaligned supervisor," "betrayed the rite," "the bond will fail."* The words were daggers, but none of them looked at me directly. Not yet.

Then came the rustle of fabric, the snap of a twig. I turned just in time to see a figure emerge from the trees—a Wilder, his aqua skin dull under the sun, his movements too deliberate, too controlled. He wore the quiet confidence of someone who had spent years hiding in plain sight. His gaze locked onto mine, and for a single breath, I felt the weight of his knowledge settle on me like a curse.

The Dohkar’s voice cut through the murmurs again. *"You stand there in silence while the contract unravels. Explain yourself before the Circle makes the decision for you."*

I exhaled through the gossamer band still curled in my fist. The stone beneath my hand was cold, but the fire in my chest was anything but. The words I needed to say would cost me everything.

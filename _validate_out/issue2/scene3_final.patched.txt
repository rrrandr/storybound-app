The air pressed against my ribs with every accusation, the scent of crushed spiralgrass and old sweat rising from the ground. Every family had turned toward me now, the youth who had offered her silence still on her knees, her Weave-Script a pale thread against her aqua skin. His slate-hued eyes found me, flecked with that brighter green near the irises, the one that always made the deck edges bite into my palm. He stood with his heels still, his frame cutting the last slant of light so the clearing dimmed by a shade. My fingers tightened around the old tarot deck in my lap, the gold leaf catching the dim light.

The threshold held me in place as the gold card warmed in my hand, the inscription holding the light in a thin unbroken line. The assembly waited, the air pressing close around the absence left by what had been taken from Seren. My heel tapped once against the stone, the sound vanishing into the hush that had held the band across my mouth. The thread the wish had opened pulled at me, a faint tug toward the card itself, the offering still present in the space between us all.Julian’s heel scraped once against the floorboards from where he stood among the observers, the sound carrying across the hush. His hand turned the ring on his finger, the metal flashing once as his thumb passed over it. “The wish bore your mark, Lirael. The alignment sang of your hand, not hers alone.”The words cut through, leaving the murmurs sharper in their wake. Murmurs rose again, sharper now, the crowd’s eyes turning from Seren to me with fresh accusation. I felt the tremor rise in my throat, the old fear that I had already explained too much and would have to explain again. Yet Julian did not wait for my denial. He held the moment open, the question beneath his voice the one my father’s leaving had taught me never to trust.“The assembly deserves the full weave,” he said, voice low and deliberate, each syllable settling with the force of an oath. “Tell them what you reached for when the rite turned.”The deck in my palm grew heavier, the gold edges pressing into my skin, the card drawing the hand toward the choice I had not made. A loose thread snagged beneath my thumb, and for one breath I could not look away from the way Julian watched me—steady, unyielding, the curve of his mouth keeping the attention fixed on what had not been asked for.I realize the deck is still in my hand. As I slide Petition Fate back in, I notice a flash of red in the otherwise gold stack. "Tempt Fate." Grandma never let me play with that one. A sword raised to a blood-red sky, lightning arcing down from the clouds (or maybe arcing up?) Ow! Static shock reminds me I need to pay attention to the present… If only it were that easy.

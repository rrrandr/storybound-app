My pulse slammed against my ribs as I stepped into the cleared space anyway, lifting my great-grandmother’s deck to begin the draw myself.My hand tightened around my great-grandmother's old tarot deck. The accusation hung in the clearing and no one moved. I got two fingers under the band at my cheek and pulled it down off my mouth, the dark auburn braid swinging against my shoulder. "It was not her wish that failed," I said, loud enough to carry.Someone near the rail repeated it to someone behind them."It was the binding that broke."I realize that old tarot deck is still in my hand.

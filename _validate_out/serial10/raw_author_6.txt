The threshold boards groaned under the Dohkar’s weight as he turned, his gaze locking on me with the weight of command. “Speak, blood of the bound line. The chains answered your silence once. Will they answer again while we stand here waiting?”

I scanned the shifting crowd beyond the rail, heart hammering for any sign of Julian’s broad frame cutting through the press of bodies. He had to be near. He always was when the Dohkar’s voice sharpened like this.

The Dohkar stepped closer, boots scraping stone. “Or do you intend to let another pay what you already owe?”

My heel caught on the edge. The memory of my lost childhood surfaced unbidden, the one I had buried beneath years of careful distance—the day the chains first claimed my name and left me nameless in return. I stepped forward, the words rising before I could swallow them. “It was not the chains that summoned them. It was the rite I tried to hold steady when Seren’s offering bent the current. The memory I lost that day was the price I carried in silence since.”

The Dohkar’s eyes narrowed, but the crowd’s murmurs thinned as if the air itself listened. No one moved to stop me. The threshold held its breath.

I realize the deck is still in my hand. As I slide Petition Fate back in, I notice a flash of red in the otherwise gold stack. “Tempt Fate.” Grandma never let me play with that one. A sword raised to a blood-red sky, lightning arcing down from the clouds (or maybe arcing up?) Ow! Static shock reminds me I need to pay attention to the present…

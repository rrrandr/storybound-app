The pale moonlight silvered the salt-crusted planks of the deck beneath my boots, turning every creak into a question I could not answer.  

The Chayr stepped closer, his voice low and edged. “You asked what the ruling will be. Then speak plainly. What lineage do the chains truly answer to, and why did you interfere with the girl’s rite?”  

My fingers twisted a loose strand of hair, the motion betraying the fear I tried to hide. The deck pressed lightly against my side as I shifted, the gold cards inside it cold and heavy. I wanted the Dohkar to look away, to let the moment pass without forcing the words I had kept buried. He did not.  

His gaze held, sharp enough to cut. The Dohkar beside him remained silent, watching.  

The Chayr’s hand rose, palm open, the gesture deliberate. “The inquiry begins now. Defend yourself or name what you have hidden.”

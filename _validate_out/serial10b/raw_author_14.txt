The flickering lanterns cast long shadows across the polished stone table as Chayr leaned in, his voice low and commanding. "You will explain the sigil now, before this assembly dissolves into whispers."

I shifted toward Seren, the deck warm against my side, but the Dohkar's gaze pinned me in place. My fingers brushed the edge of a scroll on the table, and it slipped free, unrolling across the stone. The sigil on my palm flared in answer, its silver lines matching the one drawn on the parchment—a design tied to Julian's family seal, the one he had sworn never to reveal.

Chayr's eyes narrowed, his Weave-Script flaring at his throat. "That mark binds to House Dathros. How did it come to you?"

Seren remained kneeling, her coral hair veiling her face, but her posture had not changed. The Dohkar's hand rose again, palm outward, as if to halt the moment before it unraveled further. My breath caught as the lanterns guttered once, their light pulling the sigil's pattern into sharper relief.

The deck pressed heavier against my side. It was always warm.

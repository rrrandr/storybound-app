The tapestry-draped alcove swallowed the last of the assembly's murmurs as I stepped inside, the weight of my own sigil still burning across my palm. I needed to reach Seren before the Dohkar sealed the rite entirely, to force whatever truth her wish had twisted into the open. 

Chayr's voice cut across the space before my foot cleared the fringe. "Stay where the Dohkar can see you. The rite demands it."

I turned, the seal across my mouth muffling any reply, but the sigil answered for me. It flared once, a clean pulse of light that stripped the shadows from every thread in the tapestry and threw Julian's face into sudden relief at the alcove's edge. His eyes dropped to my mouth and stayed one count too long before he spoke. 

"You came back for the truth, or for her?" The question landed like a test he already knew the answer to, his hand turning the ring on his finger once, the metal catching the new brightness. The light held, refusing to fade, and every gaze in the room shifted to the name now visible on the scroll I still carried. 

The Dohkar's band hid whatever judgment crossed his face, but the sigil's glow refused to let the moment close. I stood caught between the pull of that light and the order still hanging in the air, the choice already forming whether I named it or not.

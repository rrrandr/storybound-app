The threshold of the veil-canopy pressed cold against my bare soles, the crimson spiralgrass whispering under the weight of every eye that had turned on me. The Dohkar’s voice cut through the stillness, low and edged with command. “Name the memory you have claimed, or let the assembly witness what you have already taken.”

I searched the crowd for Julian, the silver scar at the corner of his left eye the one mark I could always find even when the rest of him stayed still. He was not there. The assembly leaned forward instead, their Weave-Script flaring in tight, accusing loops of rose and ember.

The sigil on my palm burned hotter. I stepped forward, the torn gossamer band still clutched in my fingers, and the words came before I could swallow them back. “The morning I first loved him,” I said, voice shaking, “the one I lost before the Fold took him.”

Seren’s eyes widened, the recognition that had always been hers now absent, and the Dohkar’s hand lifted as if to halt the air itself. The spiralgrass at her knees darkened further, a creeping stain that reached toward my feet. The Dohkar’s gaze sharpened on the sigil, reading what I could no longer hide.

The deck is warm. It’s always warm.

I realize the deck is still in my hand. As I slide Petition Fate back in, I notice a flash of red in the otherwise gold stack. “Tempt Fate.” Grandma never let me play with that one. A sword raised to a blood-red sky, lightning arcing down from the clouds (or maybe arcing up?) Ow! Static shock reminds me I need to pay attention to the present…

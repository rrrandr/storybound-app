The tapestry-draped alcove in the gathering room swallowed the worst of the lantern light, its heavy folds catching every flicker and turning it into long, uncertain lines across the polished stone. I stepped into that half-shadow without deciding to, the sigil on my palm still warm against my skin, the deck pressing at my side, its edge nudging against my ribs with each breath. The assembly’s eyes had followed me, but the alcove offered a moment of cover, and I took it.The cloth smelled of old incense and the faint metallic bite of Weave-Script. My fingers found the edge of the tapestry, and the silver lines across my palm answered with a sudden, sharp pulse. Light flared outward, bright enough to throw the entire room into stark relief. Every face turned. The Dohkar’s band-muffled mouth moved in a soundless protest, the only protest he ever allowed himself when the assembly watched. Chayr’s hands flattened harder on the table.Julian stood at the edge of the circle, his stillness absolute. His hazel eyes had gone green in the sudden glare, fixed on the sigil, drawn there as if pulled by its light. The scar at the corner of his left eye held the light, a thin line against the brightness.“This whole assembly is a limp-dick jerk-off that couldn’t bind a shadow,” he said, his voice low and edged, the words aimed at the Dohkar rather than me.The light from my palm did not fade. It held, steady and merciless, until the name on the unfurled scroll beneath it became impossible to ignore. Julian’s fingers turned the ring on his left hand once, the metal flashing. He did not look away from the sigil. The room waited, the cost of what had been wished already settling into the air between us.I felt the deck shift against my ribs, a quiet reminder that some truths could not be wished away twice. The sigil’s glow cast my shadow long across the tapestry, and I knew the choice had already been made for me. The only question left was whether I would speak the name aloud before the Dohkar found the will to silence the room again.As the tension thickened, the air crackled with the unspoken weight of the Arcane Binding, an ancient force that governed our actions and desires. I could almost feel its presence, a binding thread woven through the fabric of the room, urging us to confront the power dynamics at play. The sigil on my palm pulsed in response, a reminder that the choices we made were not solely our own, but tethered to the intricate laws of magic that surrounded us.
